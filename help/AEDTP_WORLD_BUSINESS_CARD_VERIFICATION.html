How To Create Your Business Card
1. Be On The Portal: Your business must first be on AEDTP WORLD BUSINESS PORTAL
2. Go To Verify: Visit /business/verify/
https://aedtpworld.com/business/verify/
3. Add Your Details:
   - Business Logo
   - Email
   - Tagline
   - What It Does
   - Business Slug: IMPORTANT! If your business name is "AEDTP WORLD" but the slug is aedtpworld, type aedtpworld as your business slug. NOT the name.
4. Generate The Card: Choose quality, image type, size, colors and whatever you want
5. Link Check: Make sure the company/business link opens to http://aedtpworld.com/business/... and opens YOUR business on AEDTP WORLD

Important Rules
- Internal Only: The business card is a card that you share everywhere. It attracts users to read about your business and visit it.
- No External Links: You CAN'T create a business card to point to external sites. It must link to http://aedtpworld.com/business/. It won’t work for external.
