AEDTP WORLD WIKI UPLOAD

Last updated: 2026-08-20T17:08:31-04:00

AWFL
AWFL aka AEDTP WORLD WIKI UPLOAD is the open way to upload created wiki pages.
Accessible at: /awfl/
https://aedtpworld.com/awfl/

What To Upload
Upload only wiki pages created from /create/. Not from other paths or platforms.
- Step 1: Go to /create/ → type → download the file
https://aedtpworld.com/create/
- Step 2: Choose the category which the wiki talks about before uploading
- Step 3: Upload the wiki page file you downloaded from /create/ to /awfl/
https://aedtpworld.com/awfl/

Strict Rules
- No Manual Edits: If you edited the downloaded file manually or on your own, then don’t upload it to /awfl/
- Original Only: Don’t upload what you didn’t download from /create/
- No Re-edits: Don’t upload to /awfl/ if you later edited the file you downloaded from /create/. Because there’s no way you would say you uploaded it and edited it through /create/ and re-downloaded it before uploading to /awfl/

Why
/awfl/ only accepts clean, original files from the CREATE system. This keeps everything safe and organized on AEDTP WORLD.
