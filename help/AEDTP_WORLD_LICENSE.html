AEDTP WORLD LICENSE
AEDTP WORLD LICENSE aka AWFL aka AEDTP WORLD FREE LICENSE is the license platform for AEDTP WORLD.

It has simple guidance on how AEDTP WORLD properties are to be used in case you’re trying to own it.. 😒

https://aedtpworld.com/license/

What AWFL Covers
- Usage Rights: How you can use AEDTP WORLD content, tools, and branding
- Sharing: What’s allowed to share, remix, or reference
- Ownership: What belongs to AEDTP WORLD vs what belongs to you as a creator
