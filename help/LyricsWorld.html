LyricsWorld
LyricsWorld aka AEDTP WORLD Lyrics is an advanced lyrics platform.
Hey! Don’t think this is any regular lyrics platform or even downgrade it taking it’s a lyrics hosting site. LyricsWorld is not a toy, never a toy.
Why? Because LyricsWorld is made for everyone to add their lyrics.
Accessible at: /lyrics/
https://aedtpworld.com/lyrics/

Account Types
Create your account whether you click on “Sign Up as An Artist”. Make sure to select the type of account you’re creating:
- Contributor: Users who suggest edits. They bring FAQs and lyrics fixing to only existing lyrics or artist
- Artist: Users who submit/add lyrics and take care of their account
- Editor: Users who edit any lyrics and bio. Editors also approve and disapprove edits. That’s the only thing they can do. But they can’t edit verified artist or lyrics[contributor][artist][editor]

Verification
- Verified Artist: Can verify any of their lyrics and they must verify to build trust. If they don’t, editors can edit it
- Ownership: Verified artists own their works
- Editor Approval: Editor accounts are first approved before they can work. But contributor and artist are auto approved or need approval to start working
- Get Verified: To get verified on LyricsWorld is a great thing. Email aedtpworld@aedtpworld.com if you need verification fast, or just keep working hard and you will get verified for being the real artist

How To Add Lyrics
1. Use Tags: Use [] for lyrics tags,  etc. These tags help readers identify
2. Artwork: Copy the link of music artwork you uploaded to Blue Melody, and paste it in the Lyrics’s image field on LyricsWorld
3. Type or Paste: Type the lyrics or paste the lyrics in case you copied it
4. Publish: Publish it
5. Verify: Verify the lyrics if you’re a verified artist on LyricsWorld
It means you linked your Blue Melody Release to LyricsWorld.[Intro][Verse][PreChorus][Chorus][Bridge][Outro][English][Rap][Korean]

Benefits
Now, AEDTP WORLD can create and improve your Google Knowledge Panel and also promote your releases and verified lyrics to the biggest platforms.
