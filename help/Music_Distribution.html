DISTRIBUTION
DISTRIBUTION aka AEDTP WORLD DISTRIBUTION and this is what most have been waiting for.

AEDTP WORLD MUSIC DISTRIBUTION is a music distribution platform which allows anyone who needs his/her music released worldwide.
The great thing: AEDTP WORLD does it free. So if anyone says to pay before acquiring distribution through AEDTP WORLD, know it’s a lie. But if it’s true, then email: aedtpworld@aedtpworld.com for confirmation.
Accessible at: /distribution/
https://aedtpworld.com/distribution/

Requirements
- Getting Paid: Available for distribution songs. You must provide your Spotify and Apple Music artist link before submitting your albums or songs
- Checkbox: You must check the GETTING PAID checkbox before uploading
- Artwork: Upload at least 3000px square artwork size for the song or album
- Files: Upload only mp3 files if possible. We don’t want to download large files
- Information: Don’t provide artist name instead of full name. Make sure you provide the exact information asked. If you filled in wrong, you won’t be taken serious again

Rules & Policy
- Take Down: If you ask to take down any release of your own, we will. But we won’t distribute your songs or albums again because we will believe all you would do is to release and take down and you’re therefore not serious. We don’t want to waste our time on what we will busily do and later remove
- Originality: Use your own beat, don’t use anyone else’s. We will check your music before distributing to avoid distributing someone’s work
- Work Ethic: If you think your music is not generating more income, work harder. Don’t think we’re the artist who will work harder for you as the boss. Work harder. Because on AEDTP WORLD, we don’t promote laziness
- Character: Be patient, honest and hardworking
- Strict: We are now strict because of past experiences with artists and upcoming ones

AI Instrumentals
Go to http://aedtpworld.com/ai/ then go to PRODUCERS section and select any AI there and generate your instrumentals. Then use the instrumental.
https://aedtpworld.com/ai/

What We Won’t Accept
We won’t accept AI voices, infringements, sex sounds. Why? Because we don’t want AEDTP WORLD to be spreading bad words.
Even when we distribute moderate explicit songs, know we’re pitying you.
We are universal, therefore we recommend more clean songs.

Communication
We will also email you on any issue or when it’s approved.
