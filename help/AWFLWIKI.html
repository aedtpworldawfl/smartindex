AWFLWIKI
AWFLWIKI aka AEDTP WORLD FREE LICENSE (AWFL) WIKI is an advanced wiki platform and system. Free and open to everyone.

Account & Permissions
- Create Account: You need to create an account to create and publish wiki pages
- No Edit: You cannot edit wiki pages after publishing
- Can Delete: You can delete your own wiki pages
- Account Deletion: Note: when you delete your account, all wiki pages created by you will also be removed instantly

Verification & Quality
Wiki Pages: Your wiki can also be notable. AEDTP WORLD or any admin can verify any wiki page when it’s notable. But it won’t be deleted that easily. It will just be verified when it’s notable.
Accounts: Your account can also be verified. But it doesn’t mean you can verify any wiki pages you made. Pages are not automatically verified just because your account is verified.
Team Support: AEDTP WORLD teams can also improve your wiki pages and fix errors in any wiki pages.

This Is Not A Toy
The platform is never a toy because all wiki platforms can be promoted to the biggest platforms including WIKIPEDIA.
