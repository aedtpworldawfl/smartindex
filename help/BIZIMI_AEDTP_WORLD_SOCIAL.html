BIZIMI
BIZIMI aka AEDTP WORLD SOCIAL `BIZIMI` is an acronym for Business Intelligence Zone Innovation Media Integration also known by its nickname "Busy Me".
It is a social media and content-sharing platform developed under AEDTP WORLD, created by Emmanuel Deliver Amable.
Accessible at: /social/
https://aedtpworld.com/social/

What BIZIMI Does
The platform allows users to upload, share, and discover videos and images, and combines elements of traditional social networking with business- and growth-oriented content discovery.

Key Features
- Accounts: Register with name, unique username, profile image, gender, country, email, and security question
- Content Upload: Upload videos via YouTube or direct links and images with title and description
- Following System: Follow and be followed by other accounts
- Verified Badge & Ranking: Upload 10 pieces of content to get verified. Every additional 10 uploads increases rank
- Likes & Trending: Like videos and images. Trending page surfaces most-liked content
- Public Access: Videos and images can be viewed without an account. Uploading, editing, liking require registration
