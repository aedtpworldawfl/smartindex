AEDTP WORLD APPS STORE
AEDTP WORLD APPS STORE is an application and software platform for uploaders, developers, and companies.

Account Types
Developers: Can create up to 100 companies, upload apps, and request verification. Developers are accounts.
Uploaders: Can upload apps. Uploaders cannot create companies. Uploaders are accounts.
Companies: Professional or business pages created by Developers. Companies are not accounts. Companies and Developers can be verified by AEDTP WORLD to show they are trusted.

Verification & Trust
Developers get verified when they are confirmed as the real developer. Companies created by Developers can also be verified.
Apps can be verified too, and it means the app is trusted to use. Verified accounts can verify their apps instantly.
Developers can submit a Request to be verified.

What You Can Do
- Download Apps: Anyone, even with no account, can download apps on AEDTP WORLD APPS STORE at /apps/
https://aedtpworld.com/apps/
- Upload & Manage: Upload apps, delete what you made, and switch into any Company page you created
- Analytics: See downloads per app and total, followers, following, and data analytics
- Engagement: Apps can be rated and commented by logged in users
- Discovery: All apps, companies, developers, and uploaders are listed on /apps/world/ for professional discovery
https://aedtpworld.com/apps/world/

Store Pages
On AEDTP WORLD APPS STORE you will find pages for Trending Apps, Trending Contents, and Videos about Productions. Note: Only production-related videos are allowed, not regular videos.

Important Rules
1. Read First: Users must mainly prioritize reading /license/copyright before starting anything.
https://aedtpworld.com/license/copyright
2. Share for Growth: Users must share their apps and company links for quick discovery and interactions.
3. Content Policy: You can get banned or deleted when bad content is found on your page or attached to your account.
and many more... Check the APPS STORE regularly for new features and policies.
