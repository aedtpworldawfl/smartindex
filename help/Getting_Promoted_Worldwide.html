Getting Promoted Worldwide

Last updated: 2026-08-21T08:24:48-04:00

BLUE MELODY + LYRICSWORLD WORLDWIDE PROMOTION

The Song Must Match
The song on Blue Melody must be the same song as on LyricsWorld.

How To Get Promoted Worldwide

1. Downloads Unlock Everything
If your song on Blue Melody gets at least 100 downloads, it will be promoted.
Note: Downloads only. Not plays. Downloads cost data so they prove real fans.

When you hit 100+ downloads we will:
1. Create or improve your Google Knowledge Panel
2. Add your song and artist data to major music databases with links back to you
3. If the same song has Verified lyrics on LyricsWorld, we also syndicate those lyrics globally with backlinks to your profiles
4. Add links to your music on major platforms

If there are no lyrics, only the song gets promoted.

Important Requirements

Image Must Match
Before uploading to Blue Melody, upload your song image first.
Copy that same image URL and use it as the lyrics image URL on LyricsWorld.
When music and lyrics are live, the image must show correctly on both.
If the image does not show, it will NOT be promoted even with 100+ downloads.
Double check your image URL.

Sharing Tips
Lyrics: Share your lyrics to get discovered and gain views.
Music: Share your Blue Melody song to get more plays and downloads.
Remember: Only downloads count toward promotion.
