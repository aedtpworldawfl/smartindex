REGISTER

Accessible at: /register/

Who Can Register
- Starter: Just starting out
- Moderate: Growing business
- Professional: Established business
You’re allowed. We accept honest and legit business. It doesn’t matter whether it’s poor or rich but must be true and honest.

Before You Apply
Important: Read /license/copyright before applying. We don’t want misunderstandings from you.

How It Works
1. Submit: Fill in your registration at /register/
https://aedtpworld.com/register/
2. Review: We will check your application
3. Launch: If accepted, we will create your online business platform you need for your legit business
4. Your Link: It will be at http://aedtpworld.com/business/..

What We Require
We don’t require what you can’t find. It’s easy. We want to help you get discovered and up we go. Don’t lose the opportunity.
Just read and fill in what you need.

Say This Instead:
"Create me a platform named..., it should have... when user clicks..."
Hope you understand what we require. Give attribution of your business you want us to build for you.
