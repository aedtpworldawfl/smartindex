AWFL WIKI CREATOR
AWFL WIKI CREATOR aka CREATE is a simple but complex way to easily make any wiki you want.
Accessible at: /create/
https://aedtpworld.com/create/

How It Works
- 1. Create: Anyone can make wiki pages through CREATE. A field will be there to type in plain text.
- 2. Download: After you download, you get your wiki page. You can host it anywhere else.
- 3. Upload to AEDTP WORLD: If you want it on AEDTP WORLD, upload only what you downloaded from /create/ to /awfl/. Then choose the category of what the subject of the wiki page is talking about.

Important Rules
- Don’t do anything stupid even though it’s simple to make
- Do not change anything in the wiki file you downloaded from /create/ if you want to upload it to /awfl/
- Free and Open: You can upload only. If it doesn’t appear to be available, we will make it available

Where To See It
To see it, go to http://aedtpworld.com and scroll down to "AEDTP WORLD FREE LICENSE". You will see all the wiki categories there. Click the one you uploaded it to.
