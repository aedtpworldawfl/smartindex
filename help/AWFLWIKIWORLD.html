AWFLWIKIWORLD
AWFLWIKIWORLD aka AEDTP WORLD WIKIWORLD is an advanced and beautiful UI wiki system. A MediaWiki type, but the problem is, it’s better looking than WIKIPEDIA.
Accessible at: /wikiworld/
https://aedtpworld.com/wikiworld/

Open & Free
- Open to Edit: Free to edit and create wiki pages
- 40+ Templates: Built-in templates to make pages look clean
- Easy Signup: Create account, get email, confirm and straight up you can create and edit unprotected pages

Admin & Rules
AEDTP WORLD will: Delete wiki pages which violate AEDTP WORLD rules, improve wiki pages, protect pages, move pages, etc.

Writing Rule - IMPORTANT
As the contributor you need to write wiki in THIRD PERSON perspective.
You won’t be banned when you write in first person, but please write in third. In fact, write in third person or else, that page will sure be removed.

About AEDTP WORLD Wikis
Note: All wiki pages or platforms on AEDTP WORLD can be promoted to biggest platforms.
AEDTP WORLD platforms are made differently. You may think they do or are the same thing "wiki" but they are made for different performances. Some are editable on devices and some are not editable easily. Try what you can. Don’t break rules.

Be Humble
Create your own page. If you think you can edit wiki pages, do it. And if you can’t, then use normal plain text. That’s even better. We will later format them.
