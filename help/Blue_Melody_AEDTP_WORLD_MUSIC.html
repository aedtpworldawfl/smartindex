BLUE MELODY
Blue Melody aka AEDTP WORLD MUSIC is an advanced and combined music platform. Think Spotify, Audiomack, Apple Music, YouTube Music... all combined as one platform named "Blue Melody (BM)".
Accessible at: /music/
https://aedtpworld.com/music/

This Is Not A Toy
Blue Melody is not a toy to be played with. Here’s why:

1. Ownership & Metadata
Any music uploaded by artists on Blue Melody, whether professional, upcoming, or starter, builds Great Metadata for each song to the specific artist. So in case someone downloads and re-uploads, it shows the owner and not the uploader.
Infringement: If you find that your song is being infringed by others on Blue Melody, contact: aedtpworld@aedtpworld.com

2. Promotion With Attribution
Any music or song on BLUE MELODY can be selected and promoted by anyone, but you must give attribution to the rightful artist.

3. Notability, Not Payment
This is the bigger one. It is not compulsory that we will make you a Google Knowledge Panel. We will pick who we want, the best trending songs and artists, and make them NOTABLE on one or many of the biggest platforms.
Important: Don’t think we will pay you, because you’re not distributing on Blue Melody, you’re uploading. AEDTP WORLD doesn’t pay you. It helps you to get what you need.
So please don’t ask us for payment. But you can ask "how can I get paid?" Simple.
Everything we do is FREE. We don’t earn money by promoting you.

How To Be Notable
You need to upload your music on Blue Melody if you want to be notable. AEDTP WORLD is different, as well as its platforms.
Think hard. Please don’t be dumb, be smart. This is an opportunity everyone needs, not wants.

Final Note
Whether you decide not to be productive, it’s not our concern.
