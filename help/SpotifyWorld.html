SpotifyWorld
SpotifyWorld is an artists discovery platform developed by AEDTP WORLD.
Note: It is NOT related to Spotify or Spotify for Artists.

SpotifyWorld is used for searching songs, artists, albums, etc.
If your music is selected or promoted as notable for GOOGLE KNOWLEDGE PANEL, searching it on SpotifyWorld is the best place to check.

Accessible at: /spotifyworld/
https://aedtpworld.com/spotifyworld/

No Login Required
Anyone can search. No account needed.
It includes all notable artists. To find your artist name there is a HUGE success!

How To Get On SpotifyWorld
1. Add Your Lyrics: Go to /lyrics/ and add + verify your lyrics
https://aedtpworld.com/lyrics/
2. Add Your Music: Go to /music/ and upload your song
https://aedtpworld.com/music/
3. Link Them: Copy the image link of the song you uploaded at /music/ and paste it as the song image in /lyrics/. That’s linking it.
4. Be Notable: This is the correct way. It makes you eligible for notability and Google Knowledge Panel

Important Notes
- Not All Songs Qualify: It’s not all your songs or lyrics that will be notable. Some, most, or all can be. But not just because you’re verified.
- It Takes Time: It doesn’t happen like magic. It takes time to be notable.
- Work Hard: Be honest and be patient.

Why It Matters
Being on SpotifyWorld means AEDTP WORLD recognizes your work as notable.
This helps build your presence and can improve your chances for a Google Knowledge Panel.

SpotifyWorld Quick Notes:
1. Purpose: Artist + Song + Album Discovery
2. Access: /spotifyworld/
https://aedtpworld.com/spotifyworld/
3. Login: Not required. Public search
4. Requirement: Link /music/ + /lyrics/ to be eligible
5. Result: Notability + Google Knowledge Panel support
6. Reality: Takes time. Not instant
