Emmanuel Deliver Amable, globally known as Buddy DML (The Trenchkid), is a Ghanaian musical force: singer, rapper, songwriter, and record producer. Signed to the innovative label AEDTP WORLD, he merges raw emotion with technical precision. The alias "The Trenchkid" symbolizes resilience, authenticity and growth carved from lived experience.
Born on April 14, 2008 in Anloga, Volta Region, Buddy grew up listening to music between ages 4 and 6, and began singing at 13. He taught himself production, sound engineering, and digital audio, quickly rising as a self-sufficient creative. In 2021, he started uploading music independently, and by 2023–2024 he stepped into the professional circuit, writing, engineering, and performing every element of his art.
Musical style: melodic flows, introspective lyricism, and a unique fusion of African rhythmic foundations with hip hop, Fein drill, melodic trap, and R&B. His songs reflect faith, motivation, perseverance, and emotional depth — often described as "therapy wrapped in rhythm".
