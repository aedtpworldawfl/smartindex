Help Center

Creating an account

Tap "Sign up" from the home page, fill in your details, and choose a security question in case you need to recover your password later.

Uploading content

Once logged in, use the Upload page. Videos must be a valid YouTube link. Images must be a direct link ending in an image file extension (.jpg, .png, .gif, .webp, etc).

Verified badges & ranks

Every 10 pieces of content you upload earns you a rank. Rank 1 unlocks the verified badge; each additional 10 uploads adds a "+1" next to it.

Following

Visit any user's profile page and tap Follow. Your followers and following lists are shown on your own profile.

Claiming an account

If an account exists that belongs to you but you don't have login access, use the Claim page, search your username, and enter the 12-character code you were given.

Forgot password

Use Recover password and answer your security question to reset it.

Deleting your account

From your Profile page, use the Danger Zone to permanently delete your account and all associated content.
