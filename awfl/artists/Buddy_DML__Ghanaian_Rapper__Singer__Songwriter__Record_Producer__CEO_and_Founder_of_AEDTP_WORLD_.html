Early Life And Background

Buddy DML was born on Monday, 14 April 2008, in Anloga, Volta Region, Ghana. He developed an interest in music at an early age, listening to songs between the ages of four and six. He began singing at the age of thirteen.

Career

In 2021, Buddy DML began uploading recorded music under the name Emmanuel Deliver DML. After approximately one and a half years, he adopted the name Emmanuel Deliver DML (Buddy DML), which was later professionally shortened to Buddy DML.

He officially went professional between 2023 and 2024, establishing himself as an emerging artist within the Ghanaian Afrobeat and alternative hip-hop scene.

Buddy DML's music often reflects themes of struggle, faith, perseverance, and personal growth, with lyrical content focused on prayer, resilience, and trust in God.

Musical Style

Buddy DML is recognized for melodic flows and introspective lyricism. His natural vocal key tone notations were recorded and measured through VirtualDJ Automix Productions.

Genre: Hip-Hop, Fein Drill, R&B, Bass, Guitar, Melodic Trap, AEDTP WORLD, World (UK Type Sound)

Breakthrough Single

Buddy DML's first widely recognized song is "Yansh Na Yansh", an Afrobeat track. The song originated from a short-edited sample discovered on TikTok, which he later remade into a full track.

The song went viral between 2024 and 2025, becoming his most streamed release.

  • First released: 18 May 2024
  • Re-released: 1 August 2025

A similar song to Yansh Na Yansh is "Nyansh Na Nyansh" by Chella.

Voice Story

Buddy DML woke up one day after having a dream, He noticed a strange change in his musical voice, his voices have become much more deeper. Whenever He tried to sing high notes, his voice then breaks. But He didn't give up, He continued playing emotional songs and singing on it, making new melodies on same rhythm.

He was on his way to the nature at night to shout and sing, since no one is there. He saw his grandma, he helped her. She told him to go home. But Buddy DML didn't stop, he kept on his journey because he felt no one understands him except GOD. He sang in high pitches and notes, it worked making his voice quality to improve.

Later his father came and laughing at him. That laugh made Buddy DML stare at his father with silence for about 10 seconds, then the father left.
