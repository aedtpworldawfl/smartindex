United Arab Emirates (UAE) is a country in the Middle East on the Arabian Peninsula. It is a federation of seven emirates: Abu Dhabi, Dubai, Sharjah, Ajman, Umm Al Quwain, Ras Al Khaimah and Fujairah.

The UAE was formed on 2 December 1971. The capital is Abu Dhabi, which is also the largest emirate. Dubai is the best-known city for business and tourism.

Economy

Oil and gas built the early wealth of the country, and today it also invests in trade, tourism, finance, technology and renewable energy.

Facts

  • Arabic is the official language and English is widely used.
  • Most people live in cities along the coast.
