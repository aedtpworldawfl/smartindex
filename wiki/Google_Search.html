Founded1998

Google Search is the web search engine from Google. People type a word or question and get a list of web pages, images, videos and other answers.

It started from research by Larry Page and Sergey Brin at Stanford University, where they created a way to rank pages by how many other pages link to them. Google was founded in September 1998.

How it works

  1. Crawling - programs follow links to find pages.
  2. Indexing - the pages found are stored in a huge searchable index.
  3. Ranking - when someone searches, the best matches are put first.

Website owners help search engines by publishing a sitemap, using clear page titles and linking pages to one another.
