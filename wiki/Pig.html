Pig

Pig
TypeMammal
Found inFarms around the world
DietOmnivore

Pig is a farm animal with a round body, short legs and a flat nose called a snout. The domestic pig comes from the wild boar.

Pigs eat plants, roots and small animals, and farm pigs are usually fed grain and vegetables. They are clever animals that can learn quickly.

Family

A female pig is called a sow, a male is a boar and the babies are piglets. A sow carries her young for about 114 days.

Uses

Pigs are raised for meat called pork. Many people do not eat pork for religious or personal reasons.

Facts

  • Pigs do not sweat much, so they cool down by lying in mud or shade.
  • Pigs have a very good sense of smell.
