Black Hole

Black Hole
TypeSpace object
Found inOuter space
GravityVery strong

Black Hole is a place in space where gravity is so strong that nothing can escape from it, not even light.

A black hole forms when a very big star dies and collapses.

Why Black

It looks black because light cannot escape it. We cannot see the hole itself, we see what happens around it.

Size

  • Small black holes are as heavy as a few suns
  • Big black holes are millions of times heavier than the sun
