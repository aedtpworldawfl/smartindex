MusicBrainz is an open music encyclopedia that collects information about artists, releases and recordings.

Notability

MusicBrainz is notable because it is used by Spotify, BBC, Google , SpotifyWorld and Last.fm as a source of music metadata.
Developed by MetaBrainz Foundation

Use in AEDTP WORLD

  • Links Mdundo and Spotify credits
