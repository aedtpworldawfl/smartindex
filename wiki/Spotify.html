Spotify is a Swedish audio streaming and media service.

History

Spotify was founded in 2006 by Daniel Ek and Martin Lorentzon. It launched in 2008.

Features

  • Music streaming
  • Podcasts
  • Playlists
  • Artist profiles with unique IDs

Notability

Spotify is notable as one of the largest music streaming platforms with over 600 million users as of 2024. It is referenced by BBC, Google and MusicBrainz.
