Meta Platforms, Inc. is an American technology company founded in 2004 by Mark Zuckerberg.

Overview

Meta Platforms owns and operates Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Threads, Messenger, Meta AI, and Reality Labs (Quest VR).

History

Founded as Facebook in 2004. Rebranded to Meta Platforms in October 2021 to focus on the metaverse and AI.
