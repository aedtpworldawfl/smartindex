Labelcaster is a music distribution platform operated by Labelcaster AB based in Stockholm, Sweden.

History

Labelcaster was established to serve independent record labels operating in the digital streaming market.

Operations

The platform operates on a contract-first model. Releases are distributed only after all rights holders and royalty participants are defined and contracts are executed.

Distribution includes delivery to Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, TikTok and other platforms across more than 200 territories.

Business model

Labelcaster operates without upfront distribution fees, retaining a percentage of royalties. Royalties are processed based on reports from digital service providers.
