What is Ghana Fashion Industry Textiles and Kente

Ghana Fashion Industry includes kente weaving, wax prints, tailoring, second-hand clothing (Kantamanto), and modern fashion designers.

Kente is Ghana's most famous fabric - hand-woven in Bonwire - Ashanti and Ewe - UNESCO Intangible Heritage.

Fashion is big cultural export - Ghanaian fashion shown in London, New York - but industry struggles vs cheap Chinese imports.

History

  • Pre-colonial: Kente woven in Bonwire - Ashanti Kingdom - 17th century - for kings - Ewe kente in Volta - Agbozume
  • Colonial: Dutch wax prints introduced - 1800s - Dutch tried to copy batik - failed in Indonesia - sold in West Africa - Ghanaians loved it - GTP founded 1966 by Dutch Vlisco
  • 1960s-1970s: Textile factories boom - GTP (Ghana Textile Printing) - Tema, ATL (Akosombo Textile Ltd) - 1967, Printex, Woodin - thousands jobs - Ghana self-sufficient in textiles - Nkrumah built factories
  • 1990s: Trade liberalization - Chinese imports flood Ghana - cheap wax - GHS 30 per 6 yards vs GTP GHS 200 - factories collapse - ATL closed, GTP struggling - 25,000 jobs lost - your parents remember
  • 2000s: Kantamanto second-hand clothing market explodes - 30,000 traders - biggest in world - $100M market - from UK, US, China - locally called Obroni Wawu - Dead White Man's Clothes - 40% of Ghana clothing needs
  • 2010s: Modern fashion designers emerge - Christie Brown (Aisha Obuobi), Osei Duro, Studio 189 (Abrima Erwiah and Rosario Dawson - Hollywood actress co-founder), Ophelia Crossland - show in Lagos, London, Paris
  • 2023: Kantamanto fire - January 2023 - market burned - 10,000 traders affected

Kente - Ghana's Heritage

  • Origin: Bonwire, Ejisu - Ashanti Region - legend: Two hunters - Kuragu and Ameyaw - saw spider web - Ananse - learned to weave - 17th century - presented to Asantehene - Osei Tutu I
  • Ewe Kente: Agbozume, Kpetoe - Volta Region - more complex patterns
  • Weaving: Hand loom - narrow strips - 4 inches wide - sewn together - one cloth takes 2 weeks to 6 months - 3 yards for wrapper
  • Meaning: Each pattern has name and proverb
- Fatia Fata Nkrumah - Fathia matches Nkrumah - symbol of unity - Nkrumah's wife Egyptian - Sika Fre Mogya - Money calls blood - greed warning - Ohene Aye Me Kuro - King has destroyed my town - protest - Abusua Ye Dom - Family is force
  • Types:
- Adwinasa - All patterns in one cloth - expensive - king only - GHS 10,000 - Sika Futro - Gold dust pattern - Oyokoman - Oyoko clan pattern
  • Price: GHS 500 for simple - GHS 10,000 for Adwinasa - tourists pay more
  • UNESCO: Kente recognized as Intangible Cultural Heritage - 2024 application
  • Tourism: Bonwire Kente Village - tourists visit - 50,000 tourists per year - buy kente
  • Challenges: Chinese printed kente - fake - GHS 100 - destroys market - real weavers cannot compete - youth not learning weaving - going to Accra for Okada

Wax Prints - GTP, ATL, Woodin

  • GTP - Ghana Textile Printing - Tema - founded 1966 - joint venture Vlisco Dutch - produces real wax - GHS 200 per 6 yards - quality - but struggling - produces at 20% capacity
  • ATL - Akosombo Textile Ltd - Founded 1967 - Nkrumah - closed many times - now struggling - was biggest - 3,000 workers in 1970s - now 200
  • Printex, Woodin - Woodin is Pan-African brand - owned by Vlisco - sells fashion made from wax - shops in Accra Mall
  • Chinese Imports - GTP design copied - printed in China - imported via Togo - smuggled - GHS 30 - same design as GTP but low quality - 80% of market - kills local factories
  • Government Tax: 2023 - government imposed 20% tax on imported textiles - to protect GTP, ATL - but smuggling via Togo continues

Kantamanto Market - World's Largest Second-Hand Clothing Market

  • Location: Accra - Central Business District - near Makola
  • Size: 30,000 traders - 5 acres - $100M turnover - biggest second-hand market in West Africa - maybe world
  • Source: Clothes from UK, US, EU, China - donated to charity - then sold to Ghana - bales - 50kg bale - GHS 1,000-3,000 per bale - traders open and sell - Obroni Wawu - Dead White Man's Clothes - local name
  • Employment: 30,000 direct, 100,000 indirect - kayayei, tailors, drivers
  • Problems:
- Waste - 40% of clothes are waste - too torn - dumped - Kantamanto is biggest waste dump - Kantamanto is environmental disaster - clothes pile at beach - Korle Lagoon - Old Fadama - The OR Foundation report - Fire - 2023 fire - burned market - 10,000 traders lost goods - no insurance - No proper structures - wooden shacks - flood - rainy season - your experience
  • Global Debate: Second-hand kills local textile industry - but provides affordable clothing - GHS 5 for shirt vs GHS 100 new - poor can afford
