Introduction

Darren Jason Watkins Jr. known as IShowSpeed is American YouTuber and streamer. Famous for high-energy IRL streams and love for Cristiano Ronaldo.

Early life

Born January 21, 2005 in Cincinnati, Ohio. Raised in Bond Hill. Attended Purcell Marian High School then Ohio Digital Learning School, graduated June 2023. Created YouTube March 21, 2016 posting NBA 2K and Fortnite with 2 average viewers.

Career

  • 2021: Viral breakout on TikTok from rage reactions, 100K to 1M in 2 months
  • 2022: Breakout Streamer of Year at Streamy Awards, released World Cup song under Warner Records
  • 2024: Europe and Southeast Asia Tours, including Singapore
  • 2025: Speed Does America Tour
  • 2026: Speed Does Africa Tour across 20+ African countries including Ghana where he received citizenship, WWE debut, and single "World Cup (Champions)" with 90M views in one month
