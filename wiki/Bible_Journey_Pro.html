BIBLE JOURNEY PRO

BIBLE JOURNEY PRO is a Bible reading application by AEDTP WORLD. Its tagline is Let Your Faith Flow • The Living Word for a New Generation.

Overview

BIBLE JOURNEY PRO allows users to read all 66 books of the Bible from Genesis to Revelation with search, filter, highlights, and theme toggle. The app displays Page 1 Genesis — The First Book of Moses, called Genesis {1:1} In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth....

Features

  • Full Bible — 39 Old Testament + 27 New Testament = 66 books + Download + About Bible Journey Pro
  • Old Testament — Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, Deuteronomy, Joshua, Judges, Ruth, 1 Samuel, 2 Samuel, 1 Kings, 2 Kings, 1 Chronicles, 2 Chronicles, Ezra, Nehemiah, Esther, Job, Psalms, Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, Song of Songs, Isaiah, Jeremiah, Lamentations, Ezekiel, Daniel, Hosea, Joel, Amos, Obadiah, Jonah, Micah, Nahum, Habakkuk, Zephaniah, Haggai, Zechariah, Malachi
  • New Testament — Matthew, Mark, Luke, John, Acts, Romans, 1 Corinthians, 2 Corinthians, Galatians, Ephesians, Philippians, Colossians, 1 Thessalonians, 2 Thessalonians, 1 Timothy, 2 Timothy, Titus, Philemon, Hebrews, James, 1 Peter, 2 Peter, 1 John, 2 John, 3 John, Jude, Revelation
  • Search — Input #searchInput placeholder "🔍 Search books or text..." — searches book name and book text
  • Filter — Select #filterSelect options: All, Old Testament, New Testament
  • In-Book Search — Toggle button 📖 In-Book: OFF/ON — when ON highlights query with <mark class="hl"> and navigates with Prev/Next
  • Highlights — Active highlight #a5b4fc, other #facc15, scrollIntoView smooth center
  • Navigation — Prev and Next buttons — if inBookMode navigates highlights, else navigates books
  • Sidebar — Hidden by default, toggle with 📚 Menu button and 📚 button, contains Old and New lists
  • Theme — Dark and light — body[data-theme="dark"] bg #071026 panel #0b1530 text #e6eef8, light bg #faf3e0 panel #f4e7c5 text #2e1d05 — stored in localStorage bjp_theme
  • Last Book Memory — localStorage bjp_last_book
  • Reader Card — Centered, book-title 22px bold, book-sub 13px muted, page-content 80% width 18px line-height 1.7 white-space pre-wrap
