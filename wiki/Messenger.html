Messenger is a messaging app developed by Meta Platforms.

Overview

Messenger is an instant messaging app built for chatting, calling and sharing and available on Android, iOS, Web and Desktop.

Features

  • Sends text messages, photos and videos
  • Makes voice and video calls
  • Has group chats and Communities
  • Sends Stickers, GIFs and Reactions
  • Helps with business with AI chats

History

Launched August 2011 as Facebook Chat, became a separate app in 2014, became part of Meta Platforms in 2021.
