Vevo is a music video hosting service founded as a joint venture by Universal Music Group and Sony Music Entertainment. It was launched on December 8, 2009 and syndicates music videos on YouTube.

Vevo Certified is an award program launched in June 2012 to honor artists whose music videos reach 100 million views on Vevo and its partners including YouTube.

Vevo App and Platforms refers to the distribution of Vevo content. Vevo originally operated its own website and mobile apps but phased them out in 2018 to focus on YouTube and smart TV platforms.

Vevo Distribution

Vevo Distribution

Vevo Distribution is the syndication system that shares advertising revenue with Google and YouTube while delivering premium music videos from Universal Music Group and Sony Music Entertainment.

Ownership

Vevo was created by Universal Music Group and Sony Music Entertainment with investment from Abu Dhabi Media Company. It later partnered with Warner Music Group for premium videos. Vevo works closely with YouTube and Google.
