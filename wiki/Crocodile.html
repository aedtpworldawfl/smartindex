Crocodile

Crocodile
TypeReptile
Found inRivers, lakes and coasts of Africa, Asia, the Americas and Australia
DietCarnivore

Crocodile is a large reptile with a long body, a strong tail, a powerful jaw and tough scaly skin. Crocodiles belong to the family Crocodylidae.

They spend much of their time in or near water, with their eyes and nostrils on top of the head so they can watch and breathe while mostly hidden. They eat fish, birds and other animals.

Family

A female crocodile lays eggs in a nest near the water. The saltwater crocodile is the largest living reptile.

Crocodiles in Ghana

In Paga in the Upper East Region of Ghana, local people treat crocodiles as sacred, and visitors can see them at the Paga Crocodile Pond. The Nile crocodile is a common African species.

Facts

  • Crocodiles are cold-blooded and warm up by lying in the sun.
  • They can stay underwater for a long time without breathing.
