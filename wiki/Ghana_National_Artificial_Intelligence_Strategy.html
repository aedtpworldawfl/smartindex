What is Ghana National Artificial Intelligence Strategy

Ghana National Artificial Intelligence Strategy is a 10-year plan from 2023 to 2033 to develop and use Artificial Intelligence (AI) in Ghana.

Launched by Ministry of Communication and Digitalisation with support from GIZ, UNESCO, and Smart Africa.

Ghana is second country in Africa after Mauritius to launch national AI strategy.

Why Ghana Needs AI Strategy

  • AI market in Africa - $3 billion by 2030
  • Ghana has talent - KNUST, Ashesi, University of Ghana AI labs
  • Need ethical AI - protect data, avoid bias
  • Solve local problems - agriculture, health, education, traffic

6 Pillars of Ghana AI Strategy

Pillar 1: Talent and Education

  • Introduce AI in basic schools and SHS curriculum - GES
  • AI programs in universities - KNUST AI Lab, UG, UCC, Ashesi, Academic City
  • Train 10,000 AI professionals by 2030 - Ghana Tech Lab, Developers in Vogue

Pillar 2: Research and Innovation

  • National AI Research Centre - at University of Ghana
  • Funding for AI research - $5 million grant
  • Support startups - NLP for Ghanaian languages - Twi, Ewe, Ga, Dagbani

Pillar 3: Data and Infrastructure

  • Ghana National Data Centre - Accra
  • Open Data Portal - data.gov.gh
  • High-speed internet for AI - Ghana.gov.gh fibre
  • Ghana Card data for AI - NIA

Pillar 4: Ethics and Governance

  • Responsible AI Network Ghana - Dr. Darlington Akogo
  • AI ethics guidelines - UNESCO Recommendation
  • Data Protection Act 2012 - Data Protection Commission
  • Avoid bias against women, rural people

Pillar 5: Sector Applications

  • Agriculture: AI for crop disease detection - Farmerline, Complete Farmer
  • Health: AI for diagnosis - Zipline data, hospital AI
  • Education: AI tutors in Twi
  • Finance: AI for fraud detection - Zeepay, Momo
  • Transport: AI traffic management - Accra

Pillar 6: Industry and Ecosystem

  • Support 100 AI startups by 2030
  • AI Park at Accra Digital Centre
  • Partnership with Google AI Ghana - Accra lab opened 2019
  • Microsoft, IBM partnerships

Key Institutions

  • Google AI Lab Ghana - Accra (2019) - First Google AI lab in Africa - opened in Accra - focuses on health, agriculture, local languages
  • Responsible AI Network Ghana - Led by Dr. Darlington Akogo - CEO of minoHealth AI, KaraAgro AI
  • KNUST Responsible AI Lab - Prof. Jerry John Kponyo - UNESCO Chair
  • Academic City University AI Center
  • Ashesi University AI Lab

Ghanaian AI Innovators

  • Dr. Darlington Akogo - minoHealth, KaraAgro AI - crop disease AI, health AI
  • Dr. George Jojo Boateng - Kwame AI - education AI tutor for science
  • Dr. Ekow Bonsa - AI for local language translation
  • Dr. Francisca Adoma - AI in agriculture

Challenges

  • Lack of large datasets in Ghanaian languages
  • High cost of GPUs and cloud computing
  • Brain drain - Ghanaian AI talent moves abroad
  • Electricity - Dumsor affects AI training
