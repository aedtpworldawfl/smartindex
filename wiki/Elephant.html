Elephant is the largest land animal in the world.

Overview

Elephant is a large wild animal built for living in herds and available in Africa and Asia in savannas and forests.

Features

  • Is very big and strong
  • Has a long trunk
  • Has big ears and tusks
  • Eats grass, leaves and fruits
  • Lives in herds and is very smart

History

Lived in Africa and Asia for thousands of years, used by humans for work, now protected in national parks.
