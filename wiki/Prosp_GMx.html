Prosp GMx: The Architect of a New Sound


Born Peter Songo on May 8, 1999, in Lagos, Nigeria, Prosp GMx is a dynamic and multifaceted force in the contemporary music scene. As a rapper, singer, and visionary hip-hop beat producer, he is not just a participant in the industry but an architect of his own distinct sound. Blending the raw storytelling of hip-hop with the infectious rhythms of Afrobeat, Prosp GMx is crafting a unique sonic identity that resonates with a generation.

His musical journey is a testament to his independence and creative control. Operating under his own label, Prosp GMx Music, he oversees every aspect of his artistry, from the initial spark of a beat to the final mastered track. This dedication to his craft has allowed him to develop a versatile style that seamlessly fuses hard-hitting rap with melodic Afro-sounds. His music is a reflection of his vibrant Lagos environment, echoing the city's energy, hustle, and rich cultural tapestry.

Prosp GMx is more than an artist; he is a creator committed to pushing boundaries. By establishing a strong digital presence, he invites the world to experience his evolving sound. His music is available across all major streaming platforms, and his visually compelling content can be found on his social channels, providing an immersive look into his creative world.

Stay connected with Prosp GMx and follow his journey as he continues to redefine the future of hip-hop and Afrobeat.
