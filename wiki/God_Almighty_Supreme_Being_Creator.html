Who is Our God

Our God is the Almighty Supreme Being - Creator of Heaven and Earth - the One who was, who is, and who is to come - Alpha and Omega - Beginning and End - without Him nothing exists.

In Ghana we call Him Onyankopon (Akan), Nyame (Akan), Mawu (Ewe), Nyonmo (Ga) - the Great One who created all - before colonialism, our forefathers knew there is One Supreme God above all gods.

He is not a small god - He is GOD ALMIGHTY - El Shaddai - Jehovah - Yahweh.
