What is AWFL?

AWFL is a global search engine. Search everything across the web, images, audio, videos, news and knowledge. AWFL means AEDTP WORLD FREE LICENSE. It is classified as a search engine, a website and a web search engine, and it is associated with Ghana. Its working languages are recorded as multiple languages, and it is written in PHP.

What AWFL means

AWFL stands for AEDTP WORLD FREE LICENSE. It is an independent search engine and entity, and it is part of AEDTP WORLD. Its official name is AWFL, and its nickname is AWFL Search.[2]

Who founded it

AWFL was founded by Emmanuel Deliver Amable.

When it started

AWFL started in 2026.[3]

What AWFL searches

  • Web — web pages from across the internet.
  • Images — pictures and graphics.
  • Audio — sounds, music and recordings.
  • Videos — videos and clips.
  • News — current news and headlines.
  • Knowledge — facts and reference knowledge about people, places and things.
  • AKP — curated knowledge panel answers shown at the top of results.
  • DATABASE — structured data answers shown at the top of results.

How it works

  1. Type what you want — enter a word, a question or a web address in the search box.
  2. Choose where to look — search All, or switch to Images, Audio, Videos or News.
  3. Get answers first — knowledge panel (AKP) and DATABASE answers appear above the normal results when they match.
  4. Open the result — click a result to go to the page, or use the WEB switch to browse an address.

Features

  • Free to use — AWFL costs nothing to use.
  • No account needed — search straight away. No sign-up and no login.
  • Quick results — results appear fast.
  • Relevant information — results and answers are ranked to match what you searched for.
