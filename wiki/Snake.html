Overview

Snake is a reptile built for crawling and hunting and available worldwide in forests, deserts and grasslands.

Features

  • Has no legs
  • Crawls on the ground
  • Has a long body and scales
  • Eats mice, frogs and birds
  • Some snakes are poisonous

History

Lived on earth for millions of years, known for its crawling and shedding skin, now lives in forests and deserts worldwide.
