What is Ghana Tech Ecosystem and Venture Capital

Ghana Tech Ecosystem is network of startups, hubs, investors, government, telcos, universities building technology products in Ghana.

Ghana is 3rd in West Africa for startups after Nigeria and Senegal but small globally - ranked 80th in world - StartupBlink 2024.

Venture Capital (VC) is small - $200M total in 10 years vs Nigeria $5B - but growing.

History

  • 2010-2013: Early hubs - MEST Africa (2008) - Meltwater Entrepreneurial School of Technology - founded by Jorn Lyseggen - Norwegian - in East Legon - first incubator in Ghana - trained 500 entrepreneurs - mPharma, Complete Farmer alumni
  • 2013: iSpace, Hub Accra launch - community hubs
  • 2014: Ghana Hubs Network formed - 20 hubs
  • 2018: Ghana Tech Lab launches with GIZ and MasterCard Foundation - $10M program - trains youth - PATH
  • 2018: Accra Digital Centre - 40 companies
  • 2020: Ghana Startup Act drafting starts - support startups - tax breaks - Startup Act passed 2024
  • 2020: COVID - ecosystem growth - healthtech, edtech, e-commerce
  • 2021: Vice President Bawumia launches Ghana.gov.gh - e-government
  • 2022: Partech Africa invests in Ghana - Complete Farmer $10M

2. Ghana Tech Lab - Accra Digital Centre and Kumasi - $10M program with MasterCard Foundation - PATH - trains 10,000 youth - makerspace, AI lab - run by MasterCard

3. iSpace - Labone, Accra - Community hub - founders: Josiah Eyison, Fiifi Baidoo - coding classes, startup support

4. Impact Hub Accra - Co-working - part of global Impact Hub network

5. Kumasi Hive - Kumasi - Makerspace - IoT for farmers - founded by Jorge Appiah - KNUST area

6. Ghana Innovation Hub - Accra Digital Centre - Government hub - supports startups under World Bank

7. HOPSA Ghana - Ho - Volta region hub

8. TamalHive - Tamale - Northern region

9. NubianVR, Developers in Vogue - Female-focused coding - Ghana Tech Lab program - 60% women

Venture Capital in Ghana

VC is small compared to Nigeria, Kenya, South Africa - the Big Four of Africa VC.

  • Total VC to Ghana 2015-2024: ~$200 Million
  • 2021: Peak year - $70M - global VC boom
  • 2022-2024: Drop - 60% drop - global winter - Ghana hit hard - cedi depreciation

Major Investors in Ghana

1. Chanzo Capital - Ghanaian VC - Founded by Eric Osiakwan - invested in 20 startups - Hubtel, Farmerline, etc. - Pan-African

2. MEST Africa - Invests $50K to $700K - mPharma biggest success - raised $35M total

3. Partech Africa - French VC - invested in Complete Farmer $10.4M Series A 2023

4. Acumen, Goodwell - Impact investors - Farmerline, mPharma

5. Savannah Fund, Ventures Platform - Early-stage - from Kenya and Nigeria - invest in Ghana

6. MasterCard Foundation - Not VC but grants - $10M to Ghana Tech Lab - PATH - jobs

7. Government: NEIP, YouStart, GEA - Grants - GHS 10K to 50K to startups - but small and political - many complaints

Ghana Startup Act 2024

Passed in 2024 after 4 years drafting - key provisions:

  • Definition of startup - less than 5 years, tech-based
  • Tax breaks - 3 years tax holiday for startups
  • Startup Fund - GHS 10M per year - to fund startups - managed by GEA
  • Labelling - startups can register - get benefits
  • IP protection
  • But implementation not started as of 2025 - fund not disbursed

Challenges - Your Daily Problems Reflect Ecosystem =

  • Dumsor - Light out kills startups - servers down - you face same
  • High Internet Cost - $5 per 1GB vs Kenya $0.50 - developers cannot work
  • Cedi Depreciation - Cedi lost 60% 2022-2023 - investors lose money - dollar cost
  • Brain Drain - Best developers move to US, UK, Canada - Japa - same as Nigeria
  • No PayPal, Stripe - Ghanaian startups cannot receive international payments easily - Paystack Ghana limited
  • Small Market - 33 million people - small vs Nigeria 220M - investors prefer bigger markets
  • Dash Scandal - Fraud damages trust - investors cautious about Ghana
