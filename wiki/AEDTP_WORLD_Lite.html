AEDTP WORLD LITE

AEDTP WORLD LITE — Fast and light version of AEDTP WORLD. Discover, connect, explore help files, wikiworld, tools and more. Works on all phones including keypad phones.

Purpose

Lightweight entry point for AEDTP WORLD ecosystem optimized for low-end devices and keypad phones.
