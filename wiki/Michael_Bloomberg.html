Who is Michael Bloomberg

Michael Rubens Bloomberg is an American business founder, politician and philanthropist. He is the founder of Bloomberg L.P., a global financial data and media company.

He served as the 108th Mayor of New York City from 2002 to 2013.

Early Life and Education

Michael Bloomberg was born on February 14, 1942 in Boston, Massachusetts. He grew up in Medford, Massachusetts.

He studied at Johns Hopkins University and earned an MBA from Harvard Business School in 1966.

Career

Bloomberg worked at Salomon Brothers investment bank from 1966 to 1981. After being laid off, he founded Bloomberg L.P. in 1981, launching the Bloomberg Terminal for financial professionals.

Bloomberg L.P. grew into a leading provider of financial data, news and analytics. The company also owns Bloomberg News.

In 2001, he was elected Mayor of New York City and served three terms, focusing on public health, education and urban development.

Impact and Recognition

Bloomberg revolutionized financial information services. As mayor, he is credited with revitalizing New York City after 9/11.

He is a major philanthropist through Bloomberg Philanthropies, supporting climate, public health and education.
