What is Ghana Internet Exchange and Internet Service Providers

Ghana Internet Exchange (GIX) is where Internet Service Providers (ISPs) in Ghana connect and exchange internet traffic locally without going through Europe.

Internet Service Providers (ISPs) in Ghana are companies that provide internet to homes, offices, and mobile phones.

Ghana has 5 undersea cables landing at Accra and Takoradi making it one of best connected countries in West Africa.

Ghana Internet Exchange (GIX)

  • Founded: 2005 - one of first IXPs in Africa
  • Managed by: Ghana Internet Exchange Association - ISPs association
  • Location: Accra Digital Centre, data centres in Accra
  • Members: 20+ members - MTN, Telecel, AT, Surfline, Busy, K-NET
  • Purpose: Keep Ghanaian internet traffic local - e.g., email from MTN user to Telecel user stays in Ghana, not via London - reduces cost and latency - faster internet
  • Traffic: 10 Gbps peak - small but growing

Before GIX, all traffic went via Europe - expensive and slow.

Undersea Cables Landing in Ghana

1. SAT-3 / SAFE (2002) - First cable - Ghana Telecom (now Vodafone/Telecel) - 340 Gbps - old and expensive
2. MainOne Cable (2010) - Nigerian company - landed in Accra - 10 Tbps - reduced cost
3. Glo-1 (2011) - Globacom Nigeria - landed in Accra
4. WACS - West Africa Cable System (2012) - 14.5 Tbps - MTN is major investor - landed in Accra
5. ACE - Africa Coast to Europe (2013) - 12.8 Tbps - landed in Accra - owned by Orange, MTN

Cables land at beach - cable stations in Accra - then fibre goes to data centres.

When cable breaks - e.g., March 2024 WACS and MainOne break - Ghana internet goes down for 2 weeks - your experience.

Internet Service Providers in Ghana

Mobile ISPs (99% of users)

1. MTN Ghana - 70% market - 28 million subscribers - MTN 4G covers 90% - first to launch 5G in 2024 - *138* data - expensive - GHS 5 per 1GB daily
2. Telecel Ghana - 20% market - former Vodafone Ghana - acquired by Telecel Group 2023 - 9 million users - 4G - *700* data
3. AT Ghana (AirtelTigo) - 10% market - merger of Airtel and Tigo - 8 million users - struggling - government owns 100% since 2021 - *100# data

Fixed Broadband ISPs (1% but important for offices)

1. Surfline - 4G LTE fixed - Accra and Tema - fast but expensive - GHS 300 per month unlimited - for offices
2. Busy Internet - 4G LTE - Accra - one of first ISPs in Ghana - 2001
3. K-NET - Satellite and fibre - for rural and enterprise - provides internet for GIFEC rural sites
4. Africa Online Ghana (Ghana.com) - Oldest ISP - 1994 - dial-up era
5. Comsys Ghana - Enterprise fibre - banks, hospitals
6. Vodafone / Telecel Fixed Broadband - Fibre to home in Accra - East Legon, Airport - GHS 250 per month
7. MTN Fibre - Fibre to home - Accra, Kumasi - GHS 300 per month - 100 Mbps

Satellite ISPs

  • Starlink Ghana - Launched August 2024 - $300 kit, $40 per month - fast - 100 Mbps - but expensive for rural - alternative during cable breaks - you added this
  • YahClick - K-NET - Satellite for rural Ghana

Internet Costs and Challenges

  • Cost: Ghana internet expensive - $5 per 1GB vs $0.50 in Kenya, $0.26 in India - ITU says 1GB should be less than 2% of income - in Ghana it's 5-7%
  • Speed: Average mobile speed - 20 Mbps - slower than South Africa - 50 Mbps
  • Dumsor: Light out affects cell towers - telcos need diesel - increases cost - your daily problem
  • Rural Coverage: 30% of rural areas still have no 4G - only 2G - GIFEC rural telephony project
  • Cable Breaks: March 2024 - WACS and MainOne break off Ivory Coast - Ghana offline for 2 weeks - exposed reliance on cables

Government Programs

  • GIFEC Rural Telephony - 2,000 rural sites - 3G/4G for villages - funded by 1% levy on telcos
  • Ghana.gov.gh fibre - NITA - National fibre backbone - 3,000 km
  • Free WiFi in Universities - 13 universities - UG, KNUST, UCC - but often not working
  • Ghana Open Data - data.gov.gh
