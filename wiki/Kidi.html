KiDi (born Dennis Nana Dwamena, 18 August 1993) is a Ghanaian singer and songwriter from Accra, Ghana.

Early life

Attended St. Anthony's School Accra, Accra Academy for Senior High School and University of Ghana where he studied Economics and Information Studies. Performed at Moonlight Café platform while at university.

Career

Won MTN Hitmaker in 2015 and signed to Lynx Entertainment. Debut single "Say You Love Me" in 2017. Released "Odo" in July 2017 which became a massive hit, remix featuring Davido and Mayorkun in December 2017. Released "Adiepena" in 2018.

Released debut album Sugar on 31 May 2019. Released Blue EP on 8 May 2020. Second album The Golden Boy released 24 June 2021 with global hit "Touch It" which debuted at number 20 on Billboard World Digital Song Sales and became the most streamed Ghanaian song on Spotify.

Has performed at Rapperholic Concert, Ghana Music Honors, December to Remember, S Concert, 61st Ghana Independence Celebration at Indigo O2 London alongside Sarkodie, Wizkid, Tiwa Savage.

Awards

  • Vodafone Ghana Music Awards – Artiste of the Year 2022, Album of the Year for Sugar 2020 and The Golden Boy 2022, Highlife Song of the Year for Odo 2018 and Enjoyment 2021, Afrobeats Artist of the Year 2021
  • 3Music Awards – Artist of the Year 2022, Album of the Year, Most Popular Song for Touch It
  • Touch It certified Platinum in India
