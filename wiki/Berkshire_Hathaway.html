Berkshire Hathaway

Berkshire Hathaway
TypeHolding company
HeadquartersOmaha, Nebraska, USA
Known forLed by Warren Buffett

Berkshire Hathaway is an American holding company based in Omaha, Nebraska. It began as a textile maker, and Warren Buffett took control of it in 1965 and turned it into a company that owns and invests in many other businesses.

Berkshire owns companies in insurance, railways, energy and consumer goods, including GEICO, BNSF Railway, Dairy Queen and Duracell. It also holds large shares in other public companies.

Buffett announced in 2025 that Greg Abel would take over from him as chief executive at the start of 2026.
