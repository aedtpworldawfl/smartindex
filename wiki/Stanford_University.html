Stanford University

Stanford University
TypePrivate university
LocationStanford, California, USA
Founded1885
FoundersLeland Stanford and Jane Stanford

Stanford University is a private research university in California, close to Silicon Valley. It was founded in 1885 by Leland Stanford and his wife Jane Stanford in memory of their son, and it opened in 1891.

Stanford is known for science, engineering, business and medicine, and many technology companies were started by its students and teachers.

Google

Larry Page and Sergey Brin met as graduate students at Stanford. Their research on ranking web pages there became the search engine that led to Google.

Facts

  • The campus is one of the largest in the United States.
  • Stanford is close to many of the world's big technology companies.
