Horse is a domestic animal used for riding and work.

Overview

Horse is a fast domestic animal built for riding, racing and farm work and available worldwide in farms and stables.

Features

  • Is fast and strong
  • Has long legs and a mane
  • Runs very fast
  • Eats grass and hay
  • Helps humans with riding and carrying loads

History

Domesticated thousands of years ago, used for travel, war and farming, now used for sport, racing and riding.
