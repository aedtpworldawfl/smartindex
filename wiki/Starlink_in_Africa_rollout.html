What is Starlink in Africa

Starlink in Africa is the rollout of SpaceX's satellite internet service across Africa. Starlink provides high-speed internet via Low Earth Orbit satellites, bypassing need for fiber or cell towers.

It started in Nigeria in January 2023 as first African country, and expanded to Ghana in August 2024.

History and Rollout Timeline

  • January 2023 - Nigeria becomes first African country with Starlink
  • February 2023 - Rwanda
  • May 2023 - Mozambique
  • June 2023 - Kenya
  • July 2023 - Zambia
  • August 2024 - Ghana approved by NCA - 16th country in Africa
  • December 2024 - 18 countries live
  • 2025 target - 30 countries including South Africa pending approval

Ghana Launch

National Communications Authority (NCA) Ghana approved Starlink in August 2024 after one year of regulatory review.

Starlink Ghana pricing as of 2024:


  • Hardware - 3,540 GHS (Standard Kit)

  • Monthly subscription - 770 GHS

  • No data cap

This is cheaper than many fiber providers in rural areas where fiber doesn't reach.

Impact on Africa

  • Rural schools in Kenya using Starlink for e-learning
  • Farmers in Rwanda accessing market prices
  • Hospitals in Zambia doing telemedicine
  • Ghana remote areas - mining sites, cocoa farms, island communities on Volta Lake now online
  • Competition forced MTN, Telecel, AirtelTigo to reduce data prices

Advantages

  • Works anywhere - desert, forest, ocean, mountain
  • No need for fiber infrastructure
  • Low latency - 25 to 60 ms vs old satellite 600 ms
  • Easy setup - 10 minutes

Challenges and Controversies

  • Expensive for average African - 770 GHS is high vs 100 GHS for MTN data
  • Regulatory issues - South Africa requires 30 percent local ownership - Starlink refused
  • Import duties on hardware
  • Environmental concerns - 7,000 satellites in orbit
  • Local ISPs losing customers

Competitors in Africa

  • Eutelsat OneWeb
  • Amazon Project Kuiper - launching 2026
  • MTN, Airtel fiber
  • Liquid Intelligent Technologies fiber
