What is Digital Ghana and Smart Cities Initiatives

Digital Ghana Agenda is government program since 2017 led by Vice President Dr. Mahamudu Bawumia to digitize Ghana's economy using Ghana Card, GhanaPostGPS, MoMo Interoperability, Ghana.gov.gh.

Goal: Formalize economy, reduce corruption, increase financial inclusion, make Ghana Beyond Aid.

Smart Cities Initiatives are projects to make Accra, Kumasi, Tamale smart using CCTV, digital traffic lights, free WiFi, drones.

History and Pillars of Digital Ghana

Launched 2017 after NPP won election - Vice President Bawumia - economist - championed digitization - called himself champion of digitization.

Four pillars:

1. Digital ID - Ghana Card (NIA)

  • National ID - one card for all - NIA issues
  • 17 million registered by 2024 - 90% of adults
  • Mandatory for SIM registration, bank account, MoMo, passport
  • Link to GRA - TIN, SSNIT, NHIS

2. Digital Address - GhanaPostGPS

  • Digital address for every 5x5m in Ghana - launched 2017 - Vokacom - GhanaPostGPS app
  • Ghana Post GPS - ZA1234 format - e.g., GA-123-4567
  • 4 million downloads but usage low - riders still call "Where are you?" - your experience
  • Used for NIA registration, ambulance

3. Digital Payments

  • Mobile Money Interoperability (MMI) - 2018 - GhIPSS - send money across networks - biggest achievement - GHS 1.2 Trillion transactions 2023
  • GhQR - Universal QR code for payments - 2020 - scan to pay - at shops - same as UPI India - but low adoption
  • Ghana.gov.gh - All government payments via portal - 2020 - you pay passport, DVLA via MoMo, card - reduces corruption - GHS 5B collected via portal 2023
  • E-Levy - 2022 - 1.5% tax on MoMo - controversial

4. Digital Government - Ghana.gov.gh

  • One portal for all government services - passport, DVLA, birth certificate, business registration - launched 2020
  • No more long queues and goro boys - theoretically
  • But still queues exist - system down often - your experience
  • Paperless port - 2017 - reduced port clearing time from 14 days to 4 days - but still challenges
  • DVLA online - driver license online booking
  • Passport online - but backlog - 100,000 passports pending 2024

Smart Cities Initiatives

Accra Smart City Project

  • Accra Traffic Management - Digital traffic lights at 30 intersections - 2020 - funded by African Development Bank - $50M - sensors adjust light based on traffic - but many not working - power out - Dumsor
  • CCTV - Accra Safe City - 1,000 CCTV cameras in Accra - National Security - to fight crime - funded by China - $100M - Huawei cameras - installed 2019 - but privacy concerns
  • Smart Waste - Zoomlion - Digital tracking of waste trucks - GPS
  • Free WiFi in Accra - Parks - Independence Square - but slow and not working often

Kumasi Smart City

  • Pilot - traffic lights, CCTV at Kejetia
  • Smart market - Kejetia Market - Ghana's biggest market - has CCTV, fire detection - after 2023 fire

Tamale and Takoradi

  • Planned - not started

Drone and Innovation

  • Zipline drone delivery - counts as smart city health
  • Ghana Post GPS integration with ambulance - National Ambulance Service - 112 - ambulance uses GPS to find location - saves lives

Achievements - Government Claims

  • Ghana ranked 2nd in Africa for e-government - UN E-Government Survey 2024 - after South Africa
  • Financial inclusion from 40% to 70% - 2014 to 2024 - World Bank - due to MoMo interoperability
  • GHS 5B collected via Ghana.gov.gh 2023 - reduced corruption - paperless port saved $100M
  • Ghana Card - 17M registered - used to clean payroll - ghost names removed - saved GHS 300M - government claims
  • Paperless port - reduced corruption

Criticism and Challenges =

  • Dumsor - Light out kills digitization - servers down - your daily problem - Ghana.gov.gh down during light out
  • Internet Cost - $5 per 1GB - expensive - smart city needs cheap internet - but Ghana expensive
  • GhanaPostGPS Issues - App not accurate - sometimes points to wrong place - 5m accuracy claimed but 20m reality - Vokacom contract controversial - $2.5M cost
  • Digital Divide - Rural areas no internet - 30% rural no 4G - only Accra, Kumasi benefit - Tamale, rural villages left out
  • Privacy - Ghana Card links all data - NIA, GRA, banks - risk of surveillance - Data Protection Commission weak
  • E-Levy - Kills MoMo - financial inclusion dropped after E-Levy - contradicts Digital Ghana
  • Implementation - Many portals down - passport portal - DVLA portal - "System down" - goro boys still exist - pay to get fast
