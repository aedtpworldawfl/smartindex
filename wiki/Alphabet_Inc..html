Alphabet Inc. is an American multinational conglomerate created in 2015 as the parent company of Google. It was founded by Larry Page and Sergey Brin to restructure Google and its other ventures.

Google

Google is the largest subsidiary of Alphabet Inc. and operates search, ads and cloud services. See main article Google.

YouTube

YouTube is the video platform owned by Google under Alphabet Inc. See main article YouTube.
