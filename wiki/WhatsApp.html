WhatsApp is a messaging app developed by Meta Platforms.

Overview

WhatsApp is a free messaging and calling app available on WhatsApp, Web and Desktop and inside Meta apps.

Features

  • Sends text messages, voice messages, photos and videos
  • Makes voice and video calls
  • Has Status, Channels and Communities
  • Helps with business with WhatsApp Business
  • Remembers context in chats with end-to-end encryption

History

Launched February 2009, bought by Facebook in 2014, became part of Meta Platforms in 2021.
