iPad

iPad
TypeTablet computer
MakerApple Inc
AnnouncedJanuary 27, 2010

iPad is a line of tablet computers made by Apple Inc. Steve Jobs introduced the first iPad on 27 January 2010, and it went on sale in April 2010.

An iPad has a large touch screen and is used for reading, drawing, video, games, study and work. It runs software based on iOS, and since 2019 it has used its own system called iPadOS.
