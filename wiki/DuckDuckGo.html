DuckDuckGo

DuckDuckGo
TypeSearch engine
Founded2008
FounderGabriel Weinberg
Known forPrivacy

DuckDuckGo is a search engine that focuses on privacy. It was founded in 2008 by Gabriel Weinberg and is based in Pennsylvania, USA.

It says it does not track what people search for or build a profile of them. Its results come from many sources, including its own crawler and other search providers.

DuckDuckGo is one of the alternatives to Google Search listed under Web search engine.
