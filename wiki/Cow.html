Overview

Cow is a gentle domestic animal built for giving milk and available worldwide in farms and villages.

Features

  • Is calm and gentle
  • Gives milk
  • Eats grass and hay
  • Has horns and a tail
  • Lives in herds on farms

History

Domesticated thousands of years ago from wild cattle, became important for milk, meat and farming, now kept worldwide.
