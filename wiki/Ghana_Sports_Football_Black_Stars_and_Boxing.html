What is Ghana Sports Football Black Stars and Boxing

Ghana Sports is dominated by football - Black Stars national team - 4 times African champions - boxing is second - Azumah Nelson - The Professor - world champion - Ghana has most world boxing champions in Africa.

Football is religion in Ghana - everyone supports Black Stars, Hearts of Oak or Asante Kotoko - arguments daily - trotro debates.

History of Football

  • 1950s: Gold Coast football - Accra Hearts of Oak founded 1911 - Asante Kotoko 1935 - rivalry - Hearts vs Kotoko - biggest derby in Africa - Super Clash
  • 1957: Ghana Football Association formed - after independence - Kwame Nkrumah built Accra Sports Stadium - 1961
  • 1960s: Golden Age - Ghana wins AFCON 1963 and 1965 - under Nkrumah - Ghana best in Africa - Real Republicans - Nkrumah's club - to win Africa
  • 1970s-1980s: Wins AFCON 1978 and 1982 - 4 times total - 4 stars on jersey - Abedi Pele emerges - 1980s
  • 1990s: Black Meteors - U23 - Olympics bronze 1992 - first African to win Olympic football medal - Black Starlets - U17 - world champions 1991 and 1995
  • 2006: First World Cup - Germany 2006 - Black Stars qualify - beat Czech Republic, USA - reach Round 16 - lose to Brazil 3-0 - Essien, Muntari, Asamoah Gyan stars
  • 2010: Best World Cup - South Africa 2010 - Quarter-finals - beat USA - Suarez handball - Uruguay - Gyan misses penalty - loses on penalties - most painful Ghana moment - entire nation cried - your memory
  • 2014: Brazil World Cup - disaster - player revolt - $3M cash flown to Brazil - in-fighting - group stage exit
  • 2022: Qatar World Cup - beat South Korea - lose to Portugal, Uruguay - group stage exit - but Inaki Williams switched nationality to play for Ghana
  • AFCON Recent: Poor - 2015 final lost to Ivory Coast - 2019 Round 16 - 2021 group stage exit - worst - 2023 group stage exit again - coach sacked

Black Stars - National Team

  • Nickname: Black Stars - from Black Star in Ghana flag - symbol of African freedom - Nkrumah
  • AFCON: 4 titles - 1963, 1965, 1978, 1982 - second most after Egypt (7) and Cameroon (5)
  • World Cup: 4 appearances - 2006 (Round 16), 2010 (Quarter-final - best), 2014 (Group), 2022 (Group)
  • Best Players Ever:
- Abedi Pele - Abedi Ayew - 3x African Footballer of Year 1991-1993 - Marseille - Champions League winner 1993 - father of Andre and Jordan Ayew - Asamoah Gyan - Baby Jet - All-time top scorer - 51 goals - 109 caps - World Cup 6 goals - Africa's top World Cup scorer - missed penalty vs Uruguay 2010 - painful - Michael Essien - The Bison - Chelsea legend - best Ghanaian midfielder ever - 58 caps - Sulley Muntari, Stephen Appiah, John Mensah, John Paintsil
  • Current Squad 2024: Thomas Partey - Arsenal - captain - Mohammed Kudus - West Ham - best player - 2023 - Jordan Ayew - Crystal Palace - Inaki Williams - Athletic Bilbao - Spanish-born Ghanaian
  • Jersey: Puma - $5M deal - Black Star jersey sells GHS 300 - fake GHS 50 Kantamanto

Ghana Premier League

  • Structure: 18 clubs - GPL - since 1956 - Hearts of Oak and Asante Kotoko dominate - 40 titles combined - Hearts 21, Kotoko 24
  • Big Clubs:
- Hearts of Oak - Accra - Continental Club Masters - 1911 - Phobia - 2000 CAF Champions League winners - only Ghanaian club to win - beat Esperance - but recent poor - 2024 finished 14th - Asante Kotoko - Kumasi - Porcupine Warriors - 1935 - Fabulous - owned by Asantehene - most supported club in Ghana - 2x CAF Champions League 1970, 1983 - but 41 years no Africa title - Medeama, Aduana Stars, Berekum Chelsea, Dreams FC
  • Stadiums: Accra Sports Stadium - 40K - built 1961 - renovated 2007 - Baba Yara Stadium Kumasi - 40K - home of Kotoko - Essipong Stadium Takoradi - Tamale Stadium
  • Money: GPL players earn GHS 2K-5K per month - low - many leave for Tanzania, Sudan for $2K per month - league sponsored by BetPawa - $6M - but clubs still broke
  • Attendance: Low - 1,000 average - because matches on TV - and Hearts/Kotoko only - no fans for small clubs - plus Dumsor and transport cost
  • Betting: Betting kills interest - youth bet on GPL - BetPawa sponsors

Boxing - Ghana's Second Sport

  • History: Bukom - Accra - fishing community - produces world champions - Bukom is boxing town - every boy boxes - Banku and boxing - Azumah Nelson from Bukom
  • Azumah Nelson - The Professor - Greatest African boxer ever - 2-weight world champion - WBC Featherweight and Super Featherweight - 39-6-2 - 1978-2008 - beat Jeff Fenech - Hall of Fame - from Bukom - built Azumah Nelson Sports Complex
  • Ike Quartey - Bazooka - WBA Welterweight champion 1994-1998 - from Bukom
  • Joshua Clottey - Grand Master - IBF Welterweight champion 2008-2009 - beat Zab Judah
  • Other Champions: Nana Yaw Konadu, Alfred Kotey, Joseph Agbeko
  • Current: No world champion 2024 - Richard Commey - former IBF Lightweight - lost title 2019 - Isaac Dogboe - former WBO Super Bantamweight
  • Gyms: Attoh Quarshie Boxing Gym Bukom, Will Power Gym, etc. - produce champions
  • Biggest Fight: Bukom Banku vs Ayittey Powers - 2014 - local rivalry - Ghana watched - Bukom Banku won

Other Sports

  • Football Women: Black Queens - women's team - not qualified for World Cup since 2007 - poor support
