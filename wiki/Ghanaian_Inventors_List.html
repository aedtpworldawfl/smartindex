What is Ghanaian Inventors List

Ghanaian Inventors List is a compilation of notable Ghanaian inventors who have created products, technologies, and solutions in Ghana and abroad.

Ghana has a long history of innovation from independence symbols to modern tech like drones, cars, and medical devices.

List of Ghanaian Inventors and Their Inventions

1. Theodosia Salome Okoh (1922-2015)

  • Invention: Flag of Ghana - designed in 1957
  • First Ghanaian woman to design national flag
  • Also invented hockey development in Ghana

2. Apostle Dr. Kwadwo Safo Kantanka (1948-)

  • Invention: Kantanka Automobile - cars, pickups, military weapons
  • Founder of Kantanka Group - Suame Magazine, Kumasi
  • Invented: Kantanka Onantefo 4x4, Otumfo SUV, electric cars, weapons, farming machines

3. Professor Francis Kofi Allotey (1932-2017)

  • Invention: Allotey Formalism - mathematical method for physics
  • World-renowned physicist
  • First to introduce computer science to Ghana - established KNUST Computer Science Department

4. Herman Chinery-Hesse (1963-)

  • Invention: SaaS and African Software - SOFTtribe, Black Star Line OS
  • Called "Bill Gates of Africa"
  • Built first Ghanaian software company in 1991

5. Dr. Thomas Mensah (1950-2024)

  • Invention: Fiber Optics Manufacturing - high-speed fiber optic technology
  • Ghanaian-American inventor - 7 patents in fiber optics
  • Worked at Corning Glass - helped make fiber optics cheap for internet

6. Professor Ave Kludze (1947-)

  • Invention: NASA Spacecraft Systems
  • Ghanaian NASA rocket scientist
  • Worked on Space Shuttle, ISS, Mars missions - 1980s to 2000s

7. Dr. Elikplim Kwabla Apetorgbor (1980-)

  • Invention: Nuclear Innovation - Ghana Research Reactor
  • Ghana Atomic Energy Commission scientist

8. Kelvin Dodzi (2000-)

  • Invention: Biometric Voting Device, Smart Security Systems
  • Young Ghanaian inventor from Volta Region
  • Built electronic voting machine for schools

9. Dr. Charles Ofori Antwi (1980-)

  • Invention: Medical Devices - Low-cost Incubators
  • Biomedical engineer

10. Philip Adongo - SolarTaxi

  • Invention: SolarTaxi Electric Motorcycles and Cars
  • CEO of SolarTaxi - electric transport in Accra

11. Gregory Kpiebaya - Agritech

  • Invention: Soya Processing Machines
  • Northern Ghana farmer innovator

12. Richard Fish - Farm Machines

  • Invention: Fufu Pounding Machine, Palm Nut Processing
