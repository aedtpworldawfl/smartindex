Who is Bill Gates

William Henry Gates III is an American business founder, software developer and philanthropist. He is the co-founder of Microsoft and one of the founders of modern personal computing.

He is also known for his philanthropic work through the Gates Foundation.

Early Life and Education

Bill Gates was born on October 28, 1955 in Seattle, Washington. He developed an interest in computer programming at an early age at Lakeside School.

He enrolled at Harvard University in 1973 but left in 1975 to start Microsoft with Paul Allen.

Career

In 1975, Gates and Paul Allen founded Microsoft in Albuquerque, New Mexico. Microsoft developed the MS-DOS operating system and later Microsoft Windows, which became the dominant operating system for personal computers.

Gates served as CEO of Microsoft until 2000 and as chairman until 2014. Under his leadership, Microsoft became one of the world's largest software companies.

In 2000, he and his then-wife Melinda French Gates founded the Gates Foundation, one of the largest private charitable foundations in the world focused on global health and development.

Impact and Recognition

Gates played a central role in the personal computer revolution. Through the Gates Foundation, he has contributed to global health, education and poverty reduction.

He has received numerous awards including the Presidential Medal of Freedom.
