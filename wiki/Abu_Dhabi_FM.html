Emarat FM

Emarat FM is the first Khaleeji radio station in the United Arab Emirates, launched in 1995. It is operated by Abu Dhabi Media Company and focuses on Khaleeji music and Emirati culture.

History

Emarat FM was created as the first station dedicated to Khaleeji music and culture for Emirati audiences.

Frequencies

  • 95.8 FM in Abu Dhabi
  • 94.9 FM in Al Ain
  • 97.1 FM in Dubai and Sharjah
  • Streaming via Emarat FM app

Programming

Emarat FM broadcasts Khaleeji music, cultural entertainment, talk shows about lifestyle and culture, and special programs for Emirati identity like Ahla Sabah and Ahla Masa.
