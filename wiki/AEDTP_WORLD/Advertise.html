ADVERTISE

ADVERTISE is the official advertising and sponsored listings system of AEDTP WORLD. It lists all active and generated ads on the platform.

Purpose

The purpose of ADVERTISE is to browse and display live sponsored ads on AEDTP WORLD. Users can advertise their brand with instant approval.

Features

  • Lists all active and generated ads
  • Cached for 5 minutes for speed (Cache-Control: public, max-age=300)
  • Expired ads including 24 hour free-trial ads are automatically dropped by get_cached_ads() and get_all_ads()
  • Displays total live listings count
  • Shows Free Trial badge for daily plan ads
  • Responsive ad grid (300px x 400px cards)
  • Pattern fallback and lazy loading images
  • Structured data ItemList for rich indexing with Schema.org

How It Works

ADVERTISE uses:

  • get_cached_ads() to fetch cached ads
  • pattern_index_for() for card patterns
  • cta_label_for() for call-to-action labels
  • h() for HTML escaping

If there are no ads, it shows a message: "No ads yet — be the first to advertise!" with a create button.

If ads exist, it renders them in a grid:

  • grid-cols-1 sm:grid-cols-2 lg:grid-cols-3
  • Each card contains title, description, image, and CTA
