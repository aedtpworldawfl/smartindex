Overview

Tiger is a large wild animal built for hunting alone and available in Asia in forests and grasslands.

Features

  • Is strong and fast
  • Has orange fur with black stripes
  • Has sharp teeth and claws
  • Hunts alone at night
  • Swims very well

History

Lived in Asia for thousands of years, known as a powerful hunter, now protected because it is endangered.
