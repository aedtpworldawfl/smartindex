Moon
TypeNatural satellite

Average distance from EarthAbout 384,400 km

Moon is Earth's only natural satellite. It travels around the Earth and shines because it reflects light from the Sun.

The Moon takes about 27 days to go once around the Earth. The shape we see changes during the month, from new moon to full moon and back. These changes are called the phases of the Moon, and a full cycle takes about 29.5 days.

Gravity and tides

The Moon's gravity is about one sixth of the gravity on Earth. Its pull on the oceans is the main cause of the tides.

Visiting the Moon

In July 1969 the Apollo 11 mission made the first crewed Moon landing. Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin walked on the surface while Michael Collins stayed in orbit.

Facts

  • The Moon has no air and almost no weather.
  • The same side of the Moon always faces the Earth.
  • The dark patches we see are old plains of cooled lava.
