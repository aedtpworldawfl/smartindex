Falcon 9

Falcon 9
TypeOrbital rocket
MakerSpaceX
First flightJune 4, 2010
ReusableYes, first stage

Falcon 9 is a two-stage rocket built by SpaceX, the space company founded by Elon Musk in 2002. It carries satellites and people into orbit.

Its first stage can fly back and land so it can be used again. The first successful landing of a Falcon 9 first stage was on 21 December 2015. The rocket burns kerosene and liquid oxygen in nine Merlin engines.

Falcon 9 launches the Dragon spacecraft, which has carried astronauts to the International Space Station since 2020. It also launches Starlink satellites, which provide internet service.
