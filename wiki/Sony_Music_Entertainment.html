Sony Music Entertainment

Sony Music Entertainment is an American global music company owned by Sony Group Corporation. It is one of the three major record companies in the world.

History

Founded in 1929 as American Record Corporation, later became Columbia Records, then CBS Records, and finally Sony Music Entertainment Inc. in 1991 after Sony acquired it.

  • Columbia Records
