Overview

Goat is a smart domestic animal built for giving milk and eating grass and available worldwide in farms and villages.

Features

  • Is smart and active
  • Gives milk
  • Eats grass and leaves
  • Has horns and a beard
  • Climbs hills and rocks

History

Domesticated thousands of years ago, one of the first domesticated animals, now kept worldwide for milk and meat.
