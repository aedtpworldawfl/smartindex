Blue Melody

Blue Melody – free global music discovery, streaming & distribution. Join the #1 music player for independent artists. Discover And Connect — Global Music Discovery, Distribution, Sharing & Streaming by AEDTP WORLD.

Purpose

Blue Melody is a music player for everyone. Global Music Discovery, Distribution, Sharing And Streaming. Platform for independent artists to upload and share music.

Features

  • Discover — Search artists, songs, genres with live search
  • Streaming — Fixed bottom player with artwork, title, artist, rewind 10s, play/pause, forward 10s, download, progress bar
  • Upload — Title, Artist auto-filled, Featuring, Genres, Album, Label, Cover Art .jpg max 2MB, Music .mp3 max 10MB with real upload progress
  • Profile — Artist Name, Username, Full Name, Gender, DOB, Genre, Label, Country, Email, Profile Image URL, Security Question & Answer
  • Auth — Log In / Create Account tabs, session in localStorage bm_session
  • Stats — Tracks, Artists, Total Plays
  • Verified Badge — Rank = floor(releaseCount/10), 10 uploads = badge, 20 = badge +1
  • Offline Play — Play Offline
  • LyricsWorld — LyricsWorld
  • AI — Cayla AI and AEDTP WORLD AI integration

Page Structure

  • Top Nav — Logo Blue Melody AEDTP WORLD, tabs Upload, Search, Profile, Help, About, Share, mobile hamburger
  • Hero — Gradient #0f1f4a to #1a2f6b to #7c3aed, title Blue Melody Music, search bar with clear button
  • Latest Releases — Grid auto-fill minmax 160px, music cards with artwork, play overlay, title, artist, genres, plays
  • Search Results — Separate section toggled on query
  • Player — Fixed bottom #0f1f4a backdrop blur, transform translateY, visible class
  • Modals — Track modal, Auth modal, Upload modal, Profile panel right -380px to open

Technical

  • Storage — API api.php?action=tracks, login, signup, upload, play, download
