Mdundo is an African music website that provides music downloads and streaming.

History

Mdundo was founded in 2012 to provide access to African music.

Purpose

Mdundo provides a platform for African artists to distribute music.

Notability

Mdundo is notable because it is one of the largest music services in Africa and works with Spotify, BBC and MusicBrainz.
