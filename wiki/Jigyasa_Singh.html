Jigyasa Singh (born 25 June 1994) is an Indian television actress best known for her portrayal of Thapki in Colors TV's Thapki Pyar Ki and Heer Singh in Shakti – Astitva Ke Ehsaas Ki.

Early life and education

Jigyasa Singh was born in Dehradun, Uttarakhand, India. She completed her schooling in Jaipur, Rajasthan. She pursued her Master's degree in Journalism from Delhi University.

Career

Singh made her television debut as Aliya Raheja in Chorre Tera Gaon Bada Pyaara. She gained breakthrough as Vaani "Thapki" Chaturvedi in Thapki Pyar Ki (2015-2017).

She later appeared in Dev 2, Nazar, and Shakti – Astitva Ke Ehsaas Ki. She reprised her role in Thapki Pyar Ki 2.

In 2025, Singh featured in the music video Sun Ranjheya. In 2026, she is gearing up for her comeback project Suraag 2.0.
