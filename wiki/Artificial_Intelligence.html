Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is technology that lets computers learn, reason, and solve problems like humans do.

AI is used in phones, websites, games, robots, and search engines. It can read text, see images, hear voices, and make decisions.

What Is Artificial Intelligence

AI is not one program. It is many programs that work together to understand data and make choices.

An AI system takes input, learns from it, and gives output.

Example:

  • Input: A picture of a cat
  • AI thinks: This looks like a cat
  • Output: This is a cat

Types of Artificial Intelligence

Narrow AI

Narrow AI is made for one job only.

  • Face unlock on phones
  • Voice assistants
  • Recommendation on YouTube
  • Spam filter in email

Most AI today is Narrow AI.

General AI

General AI is AI that can do any job a human can do.

General AI does not fully exist yet. It is still a goal for researchers.

Super AI

Super AI is AI that is smarter than all humans.

Super AI is only an idea in books and movies for now.

How AI Learns

AI learns from data. The more data it sees, the better it gets.

Machine Learning

Machine Learning is when a computer learns from examples instead of being told every rule.

Deep Learning

Deep Learning uses layers called neural networks. It is good for images, voices, and language.

Training

Training is the process of showing AI many examples.

  1. Show the AI 1000 pictures of cats
  2. Show the AI 1000 pictures of dogs
  3. AI learns the difference
  4. AI can now tell a cat from a dog

What AI Can Do

  • Write text
  • Make pictures
  • Translate languages
  • Play games
  • Drive cars
  • Help doctors find sickness
  • Help students learn
  • Answer questions
AI does not think like a human. It finds patterns in data.

What AI Cannot Do

  • AI does not truly understand like a human
  • AI can make mistakes
  • AI can be biased if its data is biased
  • AI does not have feelings
  • AI needs humans to check its work

Examples of AI

History of Artificial Intelligence

  • 1950 - Alan Turing asks: Can machines think?
  • 1956 - The term Artificial Intelligence is first used
  • 1997 - IBM Deep Blue beats chess champion Garry Kasparov
  • 2011 - IBM Watson wins Jeopardy!
  • 2016 - AlphaGo beats Go champion Lee Sedol
  • 2022 - ChatGPT becomes popular around the world
