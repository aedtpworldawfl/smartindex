What is Agenda 111

Agenda 111 is the largest health infrastructure project in Ghana's history. It was launched on August 17, 2021 by President Nana Akufo-Addo to build 101 district hospitals, 6 regional hospitals, 2 psychiatric hospitals and 1 regional hospital rehabilitation.

The goal is to ensure every district in Ghana has a district hospital and every new region has a regional hospital.

History and Background

Before Agenda 111, 90 districts in Ghana had no district hospital. People had to travel long distances for basic healthcare.

During COVID-19, the weakness in health infrastructure became clear. President Akufo-Addo announced Agenda 111 to close the gap.

Construction started in 2021 across all 16 regions of Ghana. Each hospital is 100-bed capacity with staff accommodation.

Hospitals Under Project

  • 101 District Hospitals
  • 6 Regional Hospitals for new regions - Bono East, Oti, North East, Savannah, Western North, Ahafo
  • 2 Psychiatric Hospitals - one in Middle Belt and one in Northern Belt
  • 1 Effia Nkwanta Regional Hospital Rehabilitation - Western Region

Features of Each Hospital

  • 100 bed capacity
  • Outpatient department
  • Male and female wards
  • Maternity unit
  • Emergency unit
  • Diagnostic center - laboratory, X-ray
  • Staff housing for doctors and nurses
  • Mortuary
  • 20 housing units for staff

Cost and Funding

The project is estimated at 1.6 billion USD funded by Government of Ghana.

As of early 2025, government reported about 60 percent completion rate, with some hospitals commissioned. The project faced delays due to funding and contractor issues.

Impact

Agenda 111 will:
  • Provide health access to 90 districts without hospitals
  • Create jobs for 25,000 health workers
  • Reduce pressure on Komfo Anokye and Korle Bu Teaching Hospitals
  • Improve Ghana's doctor to patient ratio
