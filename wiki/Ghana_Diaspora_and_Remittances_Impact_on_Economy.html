What is Ghana Diaspora and Remittances Impact on Economy

Ghana Diaspora is 4 million Ghanaians living abroad - out of 33M population - 12% outside - biggest in USA, UK, Nigeria, Italy, Germany - send $4.6B home per year - 5% of GDP - 3rd biggest forex after gold and cocoa - keeps cedi alive.

Diaspora also brings investment, skills, and tourism - Year of Return 2019 - 1M diaspora visited - Detty December - 100K diaspora party in Accra December - hotels full.

History

  • 1960s-1980s: Nkrumah era - Ghanaians go to UK to study - then stay - 1980s Rawlings coup - economic collapse - many flee to Nigeria, UK, USA - brain drain - doctors leave - 50% of Ghanaian doctors left
  • 1990s: Economic recovery - but migration continues - Italy - Ghanaians go to Italy - tomato farming - 100K Ghanaians in Italy - Modena, Brescia - Germany - 80K - Canada - 80K
  • 2000s: Kufuor era - Diaspora voting rights - ROPAA - Representation of People Amendment Act 2006 - diaspora can vote but never implemented - until 2024 still not voting
  • 2010s: Remittances grow - $1B 2010 to $3B 2019 - World Bank - Mobile Money helps - MTN Mobile Money allows diaspora to send directly to phone - cost reduces
  • 2019: Year of Return - biggest diaspora engagement - President Akufo-Addo invites African Americans - 1M visitors - 200 citizenships - diaspora now sees Ghana as hub - Ghana becomes Gateway to Africa
  • 2020-2023: COVID - remittances increased - diaspora sent more to help family - $4.6B 2023 despite global crisis - resilient

Remittances - $4.6B

  • Size: $4.6B 2023 - Bank of Ghana - 5% of GDP - 3rd biggest after gold ($6B) and cocoa ($2B) - more than oil - bigger than FDI ($1B) - lifeline
  • How Sent:
- Western Union / MoneyGram - old - expensive - 9% fee - $9 to send $100 - World Bank says Africa most expensive to send to - 9% Ghana vs 5% global average - Mobile Money - New - TapTap Send, WorldRemit, Remitly - send directly to MTN MoMo - 3% fee - $3 per $100 - cheaper - growing fast - 60% of remittances now via Mobile Money - MTN MoMo - Vodafone Cash - Bank Transfer - Ghana banks - GCB, Ecobank have diaspora accounts
  • Use:
- Family support - 60% - school fees, food, rent - your experience - diaspora pays school fees - building house - 80% of houses in Accra built by diaspora money - East Legon houses built by Burgers (diaspora nickname) - Burger is Ghanaian abroad - from Germany - Hamburg - Burger - Building - 20% - houses - every Ghanaian abroad builds house at home - even if not returning - status - plot of land GHS 100K Accra - house GHS 1M - diaspora builds - Business - 10% - small business - shop, taxi - but many fail - family mismanages - story - diaspora sends $10K for business - family chops money - Investment - 10% - treasury bills - 25% interest - diaspora buys T-bills - safe
  • Impact on Cedi: Remittances keep cedi alive - Bank of Ghana uses $4.6B to support cedi - without it cedi would be GHS 30 per $1 not GHS 15 - but still cedi falls - because import more than export

Challenges

  • High Cost of Sending - 9% - World Bank says 3% target - SDG - but Ghana still 9% - MTN MoMo reduced to 3% via TapTap Send - but banks still 9% - government should reduce - Bank of Ghana - but Bank makes money from fees
  • Mismanagement - Family chops money - diaspora sends $10K for house - uncle uses $5K - story common - trust issue - so diaspora now uses contractors directly - not family
  • Brain Drain - 50% doctors left - nurses - 30% nurses left - Ghana trains - UK takes - UK recruits Ghana nurses - Ghana complains - but can't stop - because pay low - Ghana nurse GHS 2K ($130) - UK nurse £2K ($2.5K) - 20x difference
  • No Voting - ROPAA not implemented - diaspora angry - 4M can't vote - but influence politics
  • Land Guards - Diaspora buys land - land guards take - police no help - biggest complaint - 2024 - government trying to fix - Land Act 2020
