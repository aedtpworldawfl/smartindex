What is Ghana AI Policy 2024

Ghana National Artificial Intelligence Strategy 2024-2034 is Ghana's first national policy framework for Artificial Intelligence. It was developed by the Ministry of Communication, Digital Technology and Innovations to guide responsible development and use of AI in Ghana.

The policy positions Ghana to leverage AI for economic growth, job creation, and solving local problems while ensuring ethical use.

History and Background

In 2023, Ghana started consultations for AI policy with stakeholders including universities, startups, and international partners like UNESCO.

Ghana became one of the first African countries to develop a comprehensive AI strategy aligned with African Union AI Continental Strategy.

The policy was drafted to prevent Ghana from being only a consumer of foreign AI but a creator of AI solutions for African languages, agriculture, and health.

Five Pillars

1. Talent and Skills Development - train 50,000 youth in AI, data science, machine learning
2. Research and Innovation - support AI research in universities - University of Ghana, KNUST, Ashesi
3. Infrastructure - national data centers, high performance computing, Ghana.gov data exchange
4. Ethics and Governance - data protection, bias prevention, responsible AI lab
5. Adoption in Key Sectors - agriculture, health, education, finance, climate

Key Focus Areas

  • AI for agriculture - crop disease detection, yield prediction for cocoa and maize
  • AI for health - diagnosis support, medical imaging
  • AI for education - personalized learning, Ghanaian languages AI tutors
  • AI for finance - fraud detection, credit scoring for mobile money
  • Ghanaian Languages - Twi, Ewe, Ga, Dagbani natural language processing
  • Data governance - Ghana Data Protection Commission oversight

Institutions Involved

  • Ministry of Communication, Digital Technology and Innovations
  • Responsible AI Lab (RAIL) - KNUST
  • Ghana Data Protection Commission
  • UNESCO Ghana
  • Ghana Chamber of Technology

Objectives

  • Position Ghana as AI hub in West Africa
  • Create 10,000 AI jobs by 2030
  • Develop AI solutions for local problems
  • Ensure ethical and inclusive AI
  • Support startups building AI products
