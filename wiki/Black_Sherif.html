Black Sherif is a Ghanaian singer and rapper born Mohammed Ismail Sherif on 9 January 2002 in Konongo, Ashanti Region, Ghana.

Early life

Born in Konongo-Zongo. Attended Konongo Zongo Islamic Basic School, Pinamang Educational Complex and Kumasi Academy. Joined Literary Group at Kumasi Academy. Studied at University of Professional Studies and University of Ghana.

Career

Started in 2019 with "Cry For Me". Gained attention in 2021 with "First Sermon" and "Second Sermon". Breakthrough in March 2022 with "Kwaku the Traveller" reaching Number 1 on Ghanaian and Nigerian Apple Music charts.

Released debut album The Villain I Never Was on 5 October 2022. Second album Iron Boy released 3 April 2025.

Performed at MOBO Awards 25th anniversary at OVO Arena Wembley, Mozama Disco Concert Accra 2022, Wireless Festival Abu Dhabi and UK, Something In The Water Festival Virginia Beach, Afronation Miami. First Ghanaian to perform at Wireless Festival UK.

Awards

Artist of the Year at Ghana Music Awards 2023 and 2026. BET Hip Hop Awards Best International Flow 2023. Best Hip Hop at Soundcity MVP Awards 2023. West African Artiste of the Year at The Headies 2023.
