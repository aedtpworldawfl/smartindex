Rabbit

Rabbit
TypeMammal
Found inMeadows, forests and homes around the world
DietHerbivore

Rabbit is a small mammal with long ears, strong back legs and a short fluffy tail. Rabbits belong to the family Leporidae, together with hares.

Rabbits eat grass, hay, leaves and vegetables. Their front teeth never stop growing, so they chew all the time to keep them short.

Home and family

Wild European rabbits live in underground tunnels called warrens. A female rabbit is called a doe, a male is a buck and the babies are called kits. Pet rabbits come from the European rabbit and often live eight to twelve years with good care.

Facts

  • Rabbits can run fast and jump away from danger.
  • They can turn their ears to hear sounds from many directions.
  • Rabbits are kept as pets and also live wild on most continents.
