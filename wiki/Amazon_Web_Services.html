Amazon Web Services

Amazon Web Services
TypeCloud computing service
OwnerAmazon
Launched2006

Amazon Web Services (AWS) is the cloud computing business of Amazon. It lets companies, schools and governments rent computing power, storage and software over the internet instead of buying their own servers.

AWS started in 2006 with services such as S3 for storage and EC2 for computing. Its data centres are grouped in regions around the world, including an African region in Cape Town that opened in 2020.

Jeff Bezos led Amazon when AWS was created, and it has become one of the most important parts of the company.
