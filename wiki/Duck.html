Overview

Duck is a water bird built for swimming and available worldwide in ponds, rivers and farms.

Features

  • Swims very well
  • Has webbed feet
  • Has a flat beak
  • Eats grains and insects
  • Lays eggs

History

Domesticated thousands of years ago from wild mallards, kept for eggs and meat, now also lives wild in ponds.
