What is Ghana Music Industry Highlife Hiplife and Afrobeats

Ghana Music Industry is one of Africa's oldest and most influential - invented Highlife music in early 1900s which influenced Afrobeats, Afrobeat (Fela Kuti), and Highlife spread to Nigeria.

Industry today is $50M but dominated by live shows, not streaming - 70% is Gospel music sales at churches.

History

  • 1900s: Highlife invented - coastal bands - E.T. Mensah - King of Highlife - Osibisa - Afro-rock - Guitar band music - Palm wine music - influenced Fela Kuti's Afrobeat - Highlife is Ghana's gift to world
  • 1990s: Hiplife invented - Reggie Rockstone - Grandpapa of Hiplife - combined Highlife and Hip Hop - rapping in Twi over Highlife beats - 1994 - hits: VIP, Obrafour, Tic Tac
  • 2000s: Hiplife boom - Ofori Amponsah, Daddy Lumba, Kojo Antwi - Azonto era 2010-2013 - Fuse ODG - Azonto dance - world hit - Azonto spread to UK
  • 2010s: Afrobeats era - Sarkodie becomes biggest rapper in Africa - BET winner 2012 - first Ghanaian - Shatta Wale dancehall - Stonebwoy dancehall - Reggae/Dancehall - VGMA - Vodafone Ghana Music Awards biggest award - since 2000
  • 2020s: Drill music - Asakaa - Kumerica - Kumasi drill - Black Sherif - Second Sermon 2021 - biggest Ghanaian hit since 2000 - 50M streams - Black Sherif is Gen Z voice - Gyakie - Forever - 2020 - Amaarae - global - Coachella
  • Gospel: 70% of market - Diana Hamilton, Joe Mettle, Celestine Donkor - churches buy music - biggest income

Genres in Ghana

1. Highlife - Ghana's Heritage

  • Invented in Ghana - 1900s - guitar band - E.T. Mensah - King - bands: The Tempos, Uhuru Band
  • Osibisa - 1970s - Afro-rock band - London - Ghanaian-British - hits: Woyaya, Sunshine Day - world famous
  • Amakye Dede - Iron Boy - still active - 40 years career
  • Daddy Lumba - Biggest Highlife legend - 30 albums - Aben Wo Ha - 1998
  • Modern Highlife: Kuami Eugene - Rockstar, KiDi - Touch It

2. Hiplife - Ghana's Hip Hop

  • Reggie Rockstone - Grandpapa - 1994 - Makaa Maka
  • Obrafour - Pae Mu Ka - 1999 - best Ghanaian rap album ever - in Twi
  • VIP - now VVIP - Ahomka Wo Mu
  • Tic Tac, Kontihene, etc.

3. Afrobeats - Current

  • Sarkodie - Biggest rapper - 2009-present - 7 albums - BET 2012, Best International Act Africa - first Ghanaian - 2M followers - SarkCess Music
  • Shatta Wale - Dancehall King - Ayoo, My Level - controversial - beef with Stonebwoy
  • Stonebwoy - BHIM Nation - Dancehall - won BET 2015 Best International Act Africa
  • King Promise, KiDi, Kuami Eugene - Lynx Entertainment - Richie Mensah - producer
  • Black Sherif - Asakaa drill + Highlife - Second Sermon, Kwaku The Traveller - 2022 - 50M streams - 19 years old when broke through - from Konongo - Gen Z icon
  • Gyakie - Forever - 2020 - daughter of Nana Acheampong - Highlife legend daughter

4. Drill / Asakaa - Kumerica

  • Kumasi drill - 2020 - Asakaa Boys - Yaw Tog, O'Kenneth, City Boy - Sore - 2021 - Kumasi Twi drill over UK drill beats - viral - Kendrick Lamar co-signed - Yaw Tog - Sore remix with Stormzy, Kwesi Arthur
  • Started in Kumasi - low cost - laptop production - viral on TikTok - TikTok is biggest promo now

5. Gospel - 70% of Industry

  • Biggest market - churches - Diana Hamilton - Adom - 2021 VGMA Artist of Year - first gospel to win - Joe Mettle - Bo Noo Ni - biggest gospel
  • Income: Churches invite - GHS 50K per show - bigger than secular shows
  • Celestine Donkor, Piesie Esther, MOG Music

Structure of Industry

  • Record Labels: Lynx Entertainment - Richie Mensah - biggest - KiDi, Kuami Eugene, DJ Vyrusky - Highly Spiritual - Kaywa - Kuami Eugene former - SarkCess - Sarkodie - Burniton Music - Stonebwoy - Shatta Movement - Shatta Wale
  • Producers: Richie Mensah, Kaywa, KillBeatz, MOG Beatz, StreetBeatz, TubhaniMuzik - Asakaa beats
  • Distribution: Boomplay - biggest in Ghana - 2M users - Audiomack, Spotify (launched Ghana 2021), Apple Music, YouTube - 90% of streams free - low revenue - GHS 0.01 per stream - artists make money from shows not streaming
  • Royalty: GHAMRO - Ghana Music Rights Organization - collects royalties from radio, TV, bars - but artists complain - only GHS 100 per year - corrupt - Rex Omar - board chair - fighting to reform
  • Awards: Vodafone Ghana Music Awards (VGMA) - now Telecel Ghana Music Awards - TGMA - since 2000 - biggest night - Charter House - award for Artist of Year - Sarkodie won 3 times

Challenges

  • Streaming Revenue Low - GHS 0.01 per stream - need 1M streams for GHS 10K - not enough - artists depend on shows - but shows affected by Dumsor - light out kills shows - generators cost
  • No Structures - No proper studios in Kumasi - only Accra - Asakaa boys record on laptops
  • Payola - Radio DJs demand pay - GHS 500 to play song - artists pay - kills industry
  • Nollywood/Naija Dominance - Nigerian Afrobeats bigger - Burna Boy, Wizkid, Davido - bigger budget - Ghanaian artists struggle to compete - but Black Sherif broke through
  • Piracy - Alaba market in Nigeria copies CDs - old - now streaming piracy - YouTube uploaders steal
  • No Music School - No proper music school - only informal

Ghana Music Abroad

  • UK - Fuse ODG - Azonto - 2013 - UK Top 10 - Ghanaian in UK
  • US - Amaarae - Sad Girlz Luv Money with Kali Uchis - Billboard Hot 100 - 2021 - first Ghanaian female
  • UK - Stormzy collaborated with Yaw Tog - Sore remix - 2021
  • Global - Black Sherif - Second Sermon - charted UK - BET Hip Hop Award 2022 - International Flow
