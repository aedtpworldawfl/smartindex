SpotifyWorld is a free online music search engine developed by AEDTP WORLD. It allows users to search for songs, artists, albums, and playlists from a clean and fast interface.

The platform is powered by MusicBrainz, an open music encyclopedia, and does not require users to create an account.

Features

  • Search for songs, artists and albums
  • Fast and lightweight interface
  • No account required
  • Free to use
  • Mobile-friendly

Technology

SpotifyWorld uses the MusicBrainz API to fetch accurate music metadata and provides direct search results to users.

History

SpotifyWorld was launched in 2025 by AEDTP WORLD as part of its collection of free web tools including AEDTP School, ImageWorld, and AEDTP World.
