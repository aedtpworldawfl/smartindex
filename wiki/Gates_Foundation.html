Gates Foundation
TypeCharitable foundation
Founded2000
HeadquartersSeattle, Washington, USA
FounderBill Gates

Gates Foundation is a large charity, formerly called the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation. Bill Gates and Melinda French Gates began it in 2000, and it is based in Seattle.

It works on global health, fighting poverty, education and development, and has funded vaccines and disease research in many countries, including in Africa.

In 2010 Bill Gates and Warren Buffett started the Giving Pledge, which asks very rich people to give most of their wealth to good causes. In 2025 the foundation said it plans to spend its money and close in 2045.
