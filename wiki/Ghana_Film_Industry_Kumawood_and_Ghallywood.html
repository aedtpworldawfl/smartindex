What is Ghana Film Industry Kumawood and Ghallywood

Ghana Film Industry has two divisions:

  • Ghallywood - English-speaking films made in Accra - romance, drama - similar to Nollywood - aimed at middle class and diaspora - shown on Africa Magic, Netflix
  • Kumawood - Twi-speaking films made in Kumasi - comedy, juju, family - low budget - fast production - aimed at mass market - sold on CDs, YouTube - Ghana's biggest film market by volume

Both face competition from Nollywood Nigeria which is 10x bigger.

History

  • 1980s: Ghana Film Industry Corporation (GFIC) collapses - state funded films stop
  • 1980s-1990s: Private producers start - Socrate Safo, etc. - Accra - English films - VHS era
  • 2000s: Ghallywood stars - Majid Michel, Nadia Buari, Jackie Appiah become famous - Africa Magic shows Ghanaian films - Ghana-Nigeria collaborations - Van Vicker, Majid act in Nollywood
  • 2006: Kumawood starts - Samuel Nyamekye - Miracle Films - Kumasi - Twi films - fast - shoot in 1 week - cost GHS 10,000 - sell 50,000 CDs at GHS 5 each - profitable - stars: Agya Koo, Nana Ama McBrown, Kwadwo Nkansah (Lil Win), Akrobeto
  • 2010s: Kumawood peak - 50+ films per year - dominates Ghanaian market - Ghallywood declines due to Nollywood competition
  • 2016: Netflix enters - Ghanaian films on Netflix - Shirley Frimpong-Manso - The Perfect Picture
  • 2021: National Film Authority (NFA) created - David Dontoh (veteran actor) as board chair - Juliet Yaa Asantewa Asante as Executive Secretary - to regulate and develop industry
  • 2022: Akwaaba Magic - DStv channel dedicated to Ghanaian films - launched by MultiChoice

Kumawood - The Mass Market King

  • Location: Kumasi - Ashanti Region - Twi language - but watched all over Ghana - even Accra - via YouTube
  • Founder: Samuel Nyamekye - Miracle Films - producer - discovered Agya Koo, Lil Win
  • Style: Low budget - GHS 10K-30K per film - 5-7 days shooting - comedy, witchcraft (juju), family drama, moral lessons - loud acting - appeals to market women, drivers
  • Distribution: Previously CD/DVD - sold at Kejetia, Makola - now YouTube - Kumawood TV on YouTube - 500K subscribers - films get 1M views in 1 week - monetized - GHS 5K per 1M views
  • Top Stars:
- Agya Koo - Veteran - biggest Kumawood star 2000s - now less active - Kwadwo Nkansah (Lil Win) - Biggest current - actor, musician, school owner - Great Minds School Kumasi - films: Azonto Ghost, Cocoa Season - also produces - Nana Ama McBrown - Started in Kumawood - now biggest female star - TV host - Onua Showtime - Akrobeto (Akwasi Boadi) - Comic actor - Real News - UTV - viral - became international meme - Emelia Brobbey, Mercy Asiedu, Vivian Jill
  • Challenges: Piracy - CDs copied - YouTube piracy - no cinema - low budget - no proper scripts - typecast

Ghallywood - The English Elite

  • Location: Accra - English - higher budget - GHS 50K-200K per film - 2-4 weeks shooting
  • Directors:
- Shirley Frimpong-Manso - Best director - Sparrow Productions - films: The Perfect Picture (2009), Adams Apples, Potomanto - won Africa Movie Academy Awards - quality films - feminist themes - Leila Djansi - US-based - films: Ties That Bind, Like Cotton Twines - Frank Rajah Arase - Ghana-Nigeria films - Hearts of Men - Pascal Amanfo - Nigerian-Ghanaian
  • Stars:
- Majid Michel - Biggest male star - now pastor and actor - acted in 100+ films Ghana and Nollywood - Van Vicker - Actor, producer - John Dumelo - Actor, politician, farmer - MP for Ayawaso West Wuogon 2024 - Jackie Appiah, Nadia Buari, Yvonne Nelson, Yvonne Okoro
  • Distribution: Africa Magic, Akwaaba Magic, Netflix, Amazon Prime - The Perfect Picture on Netflix - high quality - but fewer films - 10-15 per year
  • Challenges: Funding - high cost - no cinema culture - Ghanaians prefer free YouTube - Nollywood dominates - Nigerian films cheaper and more

National Film Authority (NFA) 2021

  • Created by Development and Classification of Film Act, 2016 (Act 935)
  • Executive Secretary: Juliet Yaa Asantewa Asante - filmmaker - Eat Drink Lagos
  • Board Chair: David Dontoh - veteran actor - 1980s star
  • Functions:
- Regulate films - classification - 18+, etc. - Support industry - Ghana Film Fund - GHS 10M - but not yet disbursed - Training - NAFTI - National Film and Television Institute - now University of Media, Arts and Communication - UniMAC - Location: Accra
  • Projects: Shoot in Ghana campaign - attract foreign films - Ghana has castles, beaches, Kakum - but no incentives yet

Cinemas in Ghana

  • Silverbird Cinemas - Accra Mall, West Hills Mall - 2 cinemas - 10 screens - ticket GHS 100-150 - expensive - only Accra middle class
  • No cinemas in Kumasi - Kumawood films never show in cinema - only YouTube
  • No film village - unlike Nigeria's many - Ghana planned but not built

Challenges =

  • Funding - No investors - banks see film as risky - government fund not working
  • Piracy - YouTube uploaders steal films - no enforcement
  • Nollywood Dominance - Nigeria produces 2,000 films per year - Ghana 100 - Nigeria budget bigger - Netflix prefers Nollywood
  • No Cinema Culture - Ghanaians watch at home on phone - data cost high - but YouTube free with ads
  • Dumsor - Light out affects shooting - generators cost
  • Brain Drain - Best talents move to Nigeria or abroad
