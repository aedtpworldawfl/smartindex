Amazon

Amazon.com, Inc. is an American multinational technology company focusing on e-commerce, cloud computing, digital streaming, and artificial intelligence.

History

Founded by Jeff Bezos on July 5, 1994 as an online bookstore in Seattle. Later expanded into the world's largest online retailer.

Business

  • Amazon.com - E-commerce platform
  • Amazon Web Services - Cloud computing
  • Amazon Prime - Subscription service
  • Whole Foods Market - Grocery retail
  • Twitch - Live streaming

Services

Amazon operates e-commerce, Prime Video, Amazon Music, Kindle, Alexa, Echo, and logistics services worldwide.
