Wolf

Wolf
TypeMammal
Found inNorth America, Europe and Asia
DietCarnivore

Wolf is the largest wild member of the dog family. Its scientific name is Canis lupus.

Wolves live and hunt in groups called packs. A pack is usually a family led by a breeding pair. They hunt deer and other large animals, and they communicate with body signals and by howling.

Wolves and dogs

The domestic dog is descended from wolves that people tamed thousands of years ago.

Facts

  • A wolf's howl can be heard several kilometres away.
  • Wolves can travel long distances every day to find food.
