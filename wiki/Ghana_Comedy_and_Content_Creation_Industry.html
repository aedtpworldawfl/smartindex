What is Ghana Comedy and Content Creation Industry

Ghana Comedy and Content Creation Industry is fastest growing creative industry - since 2015 - young Ghanaians using phones to create skits, vlogs, commentary - making money from YouTube, TikTok, brands.

Comedy started with stand-up - DKB, Funny Face - now moved to TikTok skits - Made In Ghana, SDK, Jackline Mensah.

Content creation is new job - Wode Maya - biggest Ghanaian YouTuber - 1.5M subscribers - shows Africa to world - Kwadwo Sheldon - commentary - ZionFelix - gossip.

History

  • 1990s-2000s: Stand-up comedy - Concert Party - Ghana Concert Party - old - theater - then TV - Osofo Dadzie - 1970s - Akan Drama - then Key Soap Concert Party - 1990s - Nkomode - Bob Santo - etc.
  • 2008: Ghanaian stand-up starts - DKB - Derick Kobina Bonney - first Ghanaian to do stand-up on Comedy Central Africa - Funny Face - Children President - Benson - etc. - performed at National Theatre - small crowds - GHS 20 ticket
  • 2015: YouTube era - Kwadwo Sheldon - content - commentary - Wode Maya - 2015 - started vlogging China where he studied aeronautical engineering - then Africa - now biggest Ghanaian YouTuber - 1.5M subs
  • 2018: Facebook comedians - Jacinta Ocansey - Nigerian-Ghanaian female comedian - Waris - Kalybos - Kalybos In China - 2015 film - made Kalybos star - Ahoufe - etc.
  • 2020: COVID boom - Lockdown - everyone on TikTok - TikTok launched Ghana 2020 - skit makers explode - Made In Ghana - 3 friends - comedy skits in Twi - 1M followers TikTok - SDK - Sadick - comic skits - Jackline Mensah - female - 500K followers - Erkuah Official - female - lifestyle comedy
  • 2022: Brand deals - MTN, Vodafone, Kasapreko Alomo Bitters, Malta Guinness pay creators - GHS 10K per brand post for top creators - bigger than AdSense

Industry Challenges

  • No Comedy Clubs - No place to perform - National Theatre expensive - GHS 10K rental - comedians can't afford - unlike Nigeria with many clubs
  • Low AdSense - YouTube pays $1K per 1M views Ghana vs $5K US - because advertisers pay less for Ghana audience - CPM low - creators need brand deals
  • Data Cost - $5 per 1GB - viewers can't watch long videos - creators make short 1 min videos - TikTok wins over YouTube - 10 min YouTube video costs viewer GHS 5 data - expensive
  • Dumsor - Light out kills production - need light to shoot - generators cost - your experience
  • No Manager - Creators self-manage - no agencies - brands cheat - pay late
  • Mental Health - Funny Face case - depression - pressure - trolling - Kwadwo Sheldon gets trolled daily

Government and Support

  • YouTube Creators Lab Ghana - 2022 - YouTube + Ghana - training at Accra - 100 creators
  • TikTok Creator Fund - Not in Ghana - TikTok pays only US, UK creators - Ghanaian creators not paid by TikTok directly - only gifts and brands - unfair
  • National Film Authority - Should support but doesn't - focuses on films not skits
  • Private: Wode Maya built Wode Maya Studios - free for creators - East Legon - Accra
