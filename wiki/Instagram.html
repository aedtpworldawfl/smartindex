Overview

Instagram is a social media app built for sharing photos, videos, Reels and Stories and available on Android, iOS and Web.

Features

  • Shares photos and videos on Feed
  • Makes Reels and Stories
  • Sends Direct Messages
  • Goes Live with followers
  • Helps with shopping and business

History

Launched October 2010, bought by Facebook in 2012, became part of Meta Platforms in 2021.
