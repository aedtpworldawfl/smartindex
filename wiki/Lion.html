Lion is a wild animal known as the king of the jungle.

Overview

Lion is a large wild animal built for hunting and living in groups and available in Africa and Asia in savannas and forests.

Features

  • Is strong and brave
  • Has sharp teeth and claws
  • Roars loud
  • Hunts in groups called prides
  • Lives in savannas and forests

History

Lived in Africa and Asia for thousands of years, known as king of animals, now protected in national parks.
