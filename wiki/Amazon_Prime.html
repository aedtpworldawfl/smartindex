Amazon Prime

Amazon Prime
TypeSubscription service
OwnerAmazon
Launched2005

Amazon Prime is a paid membership service from Amazon. It began in the United States in 2005 with fast, free delivery for a yearly fee.

Over time more benefits were added, such as Prime Video for films and series, Prime Music, and special deals. The service is offered in many countries, and the benefits depend on where you live.
