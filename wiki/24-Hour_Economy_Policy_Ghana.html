What is the 24-Hour Economy Policy

The 24-Hour Economy and Accelerated Export Development Programme (24H+) is a flagship economic policy of Ghana launched on July 2, 2025 by President John Dramani Mahama. The policy aims to transform Ghana into a round-the-clock productive economy operating 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

The policy is designed to tackle unemployment, boost productivity, and reduce import dependency by enabling shift-based work across manufacturing, agro-processing, health, transport, retail and tourism.

History and Background

The 24-Hour Economy was a major campaign promise of the National Democratic Congress (NDC) during the 2024 Ghanaian general election.

On June 26, 2025, Presidential Advisor Goosie Tanoh presented the final policy document to Speaker of Parliament Alban Bagbin. The official launch followed on July 2, 2025.

In December 2025, Parliament passed the 24-Hour Economy Bill into law with bipartisan support.

Three Pillars

The policy is anchored on three main pillars:

1. Production Transformation - agriculture as main anchor
2. Supply Chain and Market Efficiency - logistics and market systems
3. Human Capital Development - skills training and mindset change

Eight Sub-Programmes

  • Grow24 - modernising agriculture and agro-processing
  • Make24 - industrial and manufacturing growth
  • Build24 - infrastructure development
  • Connect24 - supply chains and logistics
  • Fund24 - financing and blended finance
  • Show24 / Show Ghana - culture, creative economy and tourism
  • Aspire24 - productivity mindset and digital skills in TVET
  • Go24 - governance and institutional coordination

Cost and Financing

The programme is projected to cost 4 billion USD. Government is committing 300 to 400 million USD as seed capital to catalyse private sector investment.

Government funding will be catalytic rather than dominant, with financing through commercial banks, development finance institutions and blended finance. Private sector commitments are already nearing 2 billion USD.

Companies that sign on will benefit from tax relief, stable electricity and enhanced security during nighttime operations.

Objectives and Targets

  • Create at least 1.7 million quality jobs within four years
  • Reduce import dependency and accelerate export development
  • Integrate agriculture, industry, finance and infrastructure into seamless value chain

Institutions Involved

  • 24-Hour Economy Secretariat
  • Presidential Advisory Group on the Economy (PAGE)
  • Bank of Ghana
  • Ghana Association of Banks
  • TVET Service Ghana
