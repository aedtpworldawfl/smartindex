META AI is an Artificial Intelligence assistant developed by Meta Platforms.

Overview

Meta AI is a generative AI assistant built on Meta's Llama models and available at meta.ai and inside WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and Messenger.

Features

  • Answers questions and searches the web
  • Generates images with Imagine
  • Helps with writing, coding and ideas
  • Remembers context in chats

History

Launched September 2023, major update with Llama 3 in April 2024.
