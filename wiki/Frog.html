Frog is a small animal that lives in water and on land.

Overview

Frog is a small amphibian built for jumping and swimming and available worldwide near ponds, rivers and wet places.

Features

  • Jumps very far
  • Swims well
  • Has long legs
  • Eats insects
  • Croaks at night

History

Lived near water for millions of years, starts life as a tadpole, now lives in ponds and wet areas worldwide.
