AEDTP WORLD is a Ghana-based global distribution and promotional services company founded by Emmanuel Deliver Amable on April 14, 2024.

It is described as a multidisciplinary creative-technology company headquartered in Ghana.

It operates under the AEDTP WORLD FREE LICENSE (AWFL) as a free, open digital ecosystem with the tagline "Discover And Connect".

The company provides distribution and promotional services for businesses, creators, and musicians.

Founder

Emmanuel Deliver Amable, born on April 14, 2008, is known professionally as Buddy DML and The Trenchkid TM. He is a Ghanaian musical artist, songwriter, and record producer. He began his entrepreneurial journey at the age of 13.
