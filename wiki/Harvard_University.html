Harvard University is a private university in Cambridge, Massachusetts. Founded in 1636, it is the oldest university in the United States. It is named after John Harvard, who gave his library and money to the school.

Harvard belongs to the Ivy League, a group of old and respected universities in the northeastern United States.

Well-known people

Bill Gates and Mark Zuckerberg both studied at Harvard and left before finishing. Zuckerberg started Facebook there in 2004. Michael Bloomberg earned his business degree at Harvard Business School.

Facts

  • Harvard has one of the biggest university libraries in the world.
  • It teaches law, medicine, business, arts and sciences.
