Louvre Abu Dhabi

Louvre Abu Dhabi
TypeArt museum
LocationSaadiyat Island, Abu Dhabi
OpenedNovember 11, 2017
ArchitectJean Nouvel

Louvre Abu Dhabi is an art museum on Saadiyat Island in Abu Dhabi, in the United Arab Emirates. It opened on 11 November 2017 after an agreement between the UAE and France.

The building was designed by the French architect Jean Nouvel. Its best-known feature is a huge silver dome made of many layers of patterned metal, which lets small spots of light fall below like sunlight through palm leaves.

Collection

The museum shows art and objects from many cultures and times, placed side by side to show how people around the world have shared ideas.

Facts

  • The dome is about 180 metres wide.
  • Part of the museum sits in shallow sea water.
