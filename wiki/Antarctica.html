Antarctica

Antarctica
TypeContinent
LocationAround the South Pole
AreaAbout 14.2 million square km
PopulationNo permanent residents

Antarctica is the southernmost continent. It lies around the South Pole and is almost completely covered by a thick sheet of ice.

It is the coldest, windiest and driest continent. The lowest temperature ever recorded on Earth, about -89.2 degrees Celsius, was measured at Vostok Station in Antarctica in 1983.

People

Nobody lives in Antarctica permanently. Scientists from many countries stay at research stations, some only in summer. The Antarctic Treaty, signed in 1959, keeps the continent for peace and science.

Wildlife

Penguins, seals and many sea birds live on the coast and in the surrounding ocean. Little grows on the land itself.

Facts

  • Antarctica holds most of the fresh water ice on Earth.
  • Winter is dark for months and summer has days when the Sun does not set.
