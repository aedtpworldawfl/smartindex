What is Ghana Chip-Embedded Passport

Ghana Chip-Embedded Passport is Ghana's new electronic passport with an embedded microchip launched on April 28, 2025 by Minister of Foreign Affairs Samuel Okudzeto Ablakwa.

It replaces the old biometric passport that has been used since 2010. The chip stores holder's facial image, fingerprints, and personal data for enhanced security and faster border crossing.

History and Background

Ghana introduced biometric passports in 2010 to meet ICAO standards. By 2024, fraud and printing backlogs became major issues.

In 2024, Ministry of Foreign Affairs announced transition to chip-embedded e-passport to reduce forgery and meet new ICAO requirements.

The new passport was officially launched April 28, 2025 in Accra. All new applications from May 2025 receive chip-embedded version.

Old biometric passports remain valid until expiry but will be phased out by 2030.

Features of New Passport

  • Embedded microchip with encrypted biometric data
  • Polycarbonate data page - cannot be easily forged
  • Facial recognition and fingerprint storage
  • E-gate compatible - can use automated gates in airports worldwide
  • Enhanced security - 10+ anti-fraud features
  • Ghanaian symbols - Black Star, Coat of Arms embossed
  • Adinkra symbols watermark

Types and Fees 2025

  • 32-page regular - 500 GHS - 5 years validity
  • 48-page regular - 644 GHS - 10 years validity
  • Expedited service - 800 GHS - 3 days
  • Premium center - 2000 GHS - 24 hours at Accra

Application Process

1. Apply on passport.mfa.gov.gh 2. Pay via Ghana.gov 3. Book appointment at Passport Application Center 4. Biometric capture - fingerprints and photo 5. Chip encoding and printing 6. Delivery via courier or pickup

Passport Application Centers - Accra, Kumasi, Takoradi, Tamale, Ho, Koforidua, Sunyani, Cape Coast, Wa, Bolgatanga.

Advantages

  • Faster immigration - e-gates at Kotoka, Heathrow, Dubai
  • Harder to forge - meets EU and US visa requirements
  • 10-year validity option reduces renewal
  • Global ranking - Ghana passport visa-free to 65 countries

Challenges

  • Price increase from 100 GHS to 500 GHS - public complaints
  • Printing delays - backlog of 30,000 in 2024
  • Need for new readers at Ghana borders
  • Diaspora centers - London, New York, Berlin need upgrade
