Penguins

Penguins
TypeBird
Can flyNo
Can swimYes
HomeAntarctica and southern oceans

Penguins are birds that cannot fly, but are very good swimmers.

Most penguins live in cold places like Antarctica, but some live in warmer places like South Africa and South America.

Food

Penguins eat fish, krill, and squid.

Life

  • Penguins live in groups called colonies
  • They lay eggs on land or ice
  • Both parents help care for the chick
