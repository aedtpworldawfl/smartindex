iPhone

iPhone
TypeSmartphone
MakerApple Inc
AnnouncedJanuary 9, 2007
ReleasedJune 29, 2007

iPhone is a line of smartphones made by Apple Inc. Steve Jobs announced the first iPhone on 9 January 2007, and it went on sale on 29 June 2007.

It combined a phone, a music player and an internet device in one, controlled mainly by touching the screen. The App Store opened in 2008 and let people install apps made by other developers.

iPhones run on the iOS operating system, which Apple updates every year.
