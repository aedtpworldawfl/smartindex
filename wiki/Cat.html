Overview

Cat is a small domestic animal built for companionship and hunting mice and available worldwide in homes and farms.

Features

  • Is independent and playful
  • Has sharp eyes and hearing
  • Climbs trees and jumps high
  • Catches mice and insects
  • Lives with humans as a pet

History

Domesticated thousands of years ago from wildcats, became a popular pet in Egypt, now lives with humans everywhere.
