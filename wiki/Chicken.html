Overview

Chicken is a small domestic bird built for laying eggs and available worldwide in farms and homes.

Features

  • Lays eggs
  • Has feathers and a beak
  • Eats grains and insects
  • Cannot fly far
  • Lives in coops and farms

History

Domesticated thousands of years ago from wild jungle fowl in Asia, now kept worldwide for eggs and meat.
