What are Ghana Tech Hubs and Innovation Labs

Ghana Tech Hubs and Innovation Labs are co-working spaces, incubators, and accelerators that support startups, developers, and entrepreneurs in Ghana.

They provide internet, mentorship, funding, training, and community for young innovators.

List of Major Tech Hubs in Ghana

1. MEST Africa - East Legon, Accra (2008)

  • Oldest and largest tech hub in Ghana
  • Founded by Jorn Lyseggen - Norwegian entrepreneur
  • Offers 1-year entrepreneurship program
  • Incubated: MeQasa, Complete Farmer, Kudobuzz
  • Pan-African - also in Lagos, Nairobi, Cape Town

2. iSpace Foundation - Labone, Accra (2013)

  • Founded by Josiah Eyison
  • Focus: hardware, makerspace, girls in tech
  • Programs: Unlocking Women and Technology (UWAT)

3. Impact Hub Accra - Osu, Accra (2014)

  • Global network - Impact Hub
  • Co-working and business support
  • Host for Ashesi, Stanford entrepreneurs

4. Ghana Tech Lab - East Legon, Accra (2016)

  • Focus: AI, mobile app, web development
  • Partnership with MasterCard Foundation and World Bank
  • Trained 10,000+ youth under PATH program

5. Kumasi Hive - Kumasi (2016)

  • Leading hub in Ashanti Region
  • Founded by Jorge Appiah
  • Focus: hardware innovation, fabrication lab
  • Built: Kumasi Shoe FabLab, AgriTech projects

6. Ho Node - Ho, Volta Region (2016)

  • Founded by Courage Asase
  • Supports youth in Volta Region - edtech, agritech
  • Partner of Ghana Hubs Network

7. hapaSpace - Kumasi (2016)

  • Incubator and co-working in KNUST area
  • Focus: student startups

8. Developers in Vogue - Accra (2017)

  • Women-only coding hub
  • Founded by Ivy Barley
  • Trains women in coding, AI, data science

9. Code Coast / Duapa Workspace - Takoradi (2018)

  • Leading hub in Western Region - Takoradi
  • Supports oil and gas tech, logistics

10. Tamale Hub / Soronko - Tamale

  • Hubs in Northern Region promoting digital literacy

11. Ghana Innovation Hub - Accra Digital Centre (2018)

  • Government hub inside Accra Digital Centre
  • Managed by Ministry of Communication

12. YISON Tech Hub - Wa (2016)

  • Upper West Region - youth entrepreneurship

Ghana Hubs Network

Ghana Hubs Network formed in 2017 to connect all hubs.

Chair: Prince Boakye - iSpace
Members: 30+ hubs across 10 regions
Support from GIZ, British Council

Activities:


  • Annual Ghana Hubs Network Gathering

  • Base Innovation Program

  • Policy advocacy for Startup Act

Programs and Impact

  • World Bank Ghana Jobs and Skills Project - funding for hubs
  • GIZ Make-IT in Africa - accelerator support
  • NEIP Hubs support - 50 hubs funded in 2020
  • MasterCard Foundation Young Africa Works - training

Trained over 100,000 youth in coding, entrepreneurship, digital marketing from 2016-2025.

Challenges

  • High rent and internet cost in Accra
  • Sustainability - many hubs depend on donor funding
  • Need more hubs outside Accra and Kumasi
  • Electricity - light out affects operations
