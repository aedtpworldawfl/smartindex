What is Ghana Tourism Industry Cape Coast Elmina and Kakum

Ghana Tourism Industry is Ghana's 4th biggest foreign exchange earner - after gold, cocoa, oil - 1.2M tourists 2023 - famous for slave castles Cape Coast and Elmina - Kakum Canopy Walk - Mole elephants - and Year of Return 2019 which brought African Americans - Detty December party season.

Ghana is safest country in West Africa for tourism - ranked by Gallup.

History

  • 1482: Elmina Castle built by Portuguese - oldest European building in Sub-Saharan Africa - slave trade - UNESCO World Heritage
  • 1653: Cape Coast Castle built by Swedes, then British - slave trade center - 40% of slaves passed through Cape Coast - Door of No Return
  • 1957: Kwame Nkrumah builds tourism - Ghana Museum - National Museum Accra 1957 - Kwame Nkrumah Mausoleum - but tourism small
  • 1992: Kakum National Park canopy walk built - 1992 - by Canadian NGO - 350m long - 40m high - first in Africa - becomes Ghana's most visited tourist site - 200K visitors per year
  • 1998: Emancipation Day - Panafest - festival of African diaspora - Cape Coast - to remember slavery - every 2 years
  • 2019: Year of Return - biggest tourism campaign - Ghana government - President Akufo-Addo - invited African Americans to return - 400 years since first slave ship 1619 - 1M visitors 2019 - celebrities - Steve Harvey, Cardi B, Beyonce mother Tina, Chance The Rapper - $1.9B revenue - biggest ever - Ghana became African American hub
  • 2020-2022: COVID - tourism collapsed - 0.2M 2020 - recovered 2022
  • 2022: Beyond The Return - 10-year campaign - continuation of Year of Return - Detty December - December in GH - Afronation, Afrochella - 100K diaspora in December - party - Accra becomes party capital - hotels fully booked December - price x3

Top Tourist Sites

1. Cape Coast Castle - Central Region

  • Built: 1653 - Swedes - then British - UNESCO World Heritage 1979
  • History: Slave castle - 1,500 slaves held at a time - male dungeon - female dungeon - Door of No Return - slaves left through door to ships - never returned - Governor's quarters upstairs - church above dungeon - hypocrisy
  • Visits: 100K per year - $10 entry - $7 for Ghanaian - biggest historical site - Obama visited 2009 - with Michelle - emotional
  • Location: Cape Coast - 2 hours from Accra - 1 hour from Elmina - near Kakum
  • Museum: Displays chains, shackles - story of slavery

2. Elmina Castle - St. George's Castle

  • Built: 1482 - Portuguese - oldest European building in Sub-Saharan Africa - UNESCO
  • History: First slave castle - built for gold then slaves - Portuguese, Dutch, British held it - 500 years old
  • Visits: 80K per year - $10 - less visited than Cape Coast but older
  • Fishing: Elmina fishing harbor next to castle - colorful boats - fishermen - tourists love

3. Kakum National Park - Canopy Walk

  • Location: 30km from Cape Coast - Central Region - 375 sq km rainforest
  • Canopy Walk: 350m long - 7 bridges - 40m high above forest - first canopy walk in Africa - built 1992 by Canadians - Ghana's most visited attraction - 200K visitors per year - $10 entry - scary but safe - shakes
  • Wildlife: Forest elephants (rarely seen), monkeys, 300 bird species - but tourists come for canopy walk not animals
  • Experience: 2 hour tour - guide - learn about forest - medicinal plants - 40m high view

4. Other Major Sites

  • Kwame Nkrumah Memorial Park - Accra - Renovated 2023 - $3.5M - Mausoleum of Nkrumah - first President - biggest park in Accra - 200K visitors 2023 after renovation - ticket GHS 100
  • Wli Falls - Volta Region - Tallest waterfall in West Africa - 80m high - near Togo border - 30K visitors - hike 1 hour
  • Mole National Park - Savannah Region - Biggest park - 4,840 sq km - elephants - 93 mammals - safari - you can walk with elephants - 20K visitors - $15 entry - Mole Motel inside park - stay overnight - elephants come to motel - unique
  • Nzulezo Stilt Village - Western Region - Village on stilts - on Lake Tadane - UNESCO - 500 people live on water - boat ride
  • Busua Beach - Western Region - Surf beach - expats - Busua - near Dixcove - surfing
  • Larabanga Mosque - Savannah - Oldest mosque in Ghana - 1421 - mud mosque - near Mole
  • Aburi Botanical Gardens - Eastern - 1890 - British built - 64 hectares - 1 hour from Accra

Year of Return and Detty December

Year of Return 2019

  • Idea: President Akufo-Addo - 2018 - US Congressional Black Caucus - invite diaspora - 400 years since first African slaves arrived Virginia 1619 - come home
  • Impact: 1M visitors 2019 vs 900K 2018 - 10% increase - but revenue $1.9B vs $1.2B - 58% increase - because diaspora spends more - celebrities came - Steve Harvey - Family Feud Africa - Naomi Campbell, Cardi B, Ludacross, Chance The Rapper, Beyonce's mother
  • Citizenship: 200 African Americans given Ghana citizenship 2019 - including Stevie Wonder 2024 - Louis Farrakhan - etc.
  • Branding: Ghana became Africa's leading diaspora destination - beating Ethiopia, Senegal

Beyond The Return and Detty December

  • Beyond The Return: 2020-2030 - 10-year project - continue Year of Return
  • Detty December: December in GH - party season - December 2022 - 100K diaspora came - Accra hotels full - $200 per night normal - $600 December - events:
- Afrochella - Now AfroFuture - Biggest festival - 30K attendees - El Wak Stadium - music - Burna Boy, Davido performed - Afro Nation Ghana - 15K attendees - beach festival - at Laboma Beach - Taste of Ghana, Polo Beach Club, Sandbox
  • Impact: December is now peak - Ghana makes 30% of annual tourism revenue in December - but locals complain - prices too high - traffic - Accra jam December

  • Challenges:
- Kotoka Airport - small - 3M capacity - needs expansion - $300M project planned - Roads - bad - to Mole - 8 hour drive from Accra - road bad - tourists complain - no flights to Mole - only road - Sanitation - beaches dirty - Korle Lagoon - plastic - Laboma Beach dirty after December - Price - Ghana expensive vs Kenya, Tanzania - hotel $200 Ghana vs $100 Kenya - flight to Ghana $1,200 from US vs $800 to Kenya - Dumsor - light out at hotels - generators - but noisy - Visa - $100 visa - expensive vs Rwanda $30 - but visa on arrival now
