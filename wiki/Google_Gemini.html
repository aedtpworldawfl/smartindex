Overview

Google Gemini is a generative AI assistant built on Google's Gemini models and available on Web, Android and iOS and inside Google apps.

Features

  • Answers questions and searches the web
  • Generates images and videos
  • Helps with writing, coding and ideas
  • Works with Gmail, Docs and Search
  • Remembers context in chats

History

Launched December 2023 as Bard, renamed to Gemini in February 2024, major update with Gemini 1.5 and Gemini 2.
