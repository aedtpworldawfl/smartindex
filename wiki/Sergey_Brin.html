Who is Sergey Brin

Sergey Mikhaylovich Brin is an American business founder and computer scientist. He is the co-founder of Google and co-founder of Alphabet Inc. with Larry Page.

He co-developed the PageRank search algorithm.

Early Life and Education

Sergey Brin was born on August 21, 1973 in Moscow, Soviet Union. His family emigrated to the United States in 1979.

He studied at the University of Maryland, College Park and later earned a master's degree at Stanford University where he met Larry Page.

Career

In 1998, Brin and Larry Page founded Google from a garage in Menlo Park, California. Google became the world's most used search engine.

Brin served as President of Alphabet Inc. from 2015 to 2019. He has also worked on special projects including Google X and Google Glass.

He is involved in philanthropy and technology investments.

Impact and Recognition

Brin helped transform how information is organized and accessed online. Google's innovations influenced search, advertising, and cloud computing.

He has been listed among the richest and most influential people in technology.
