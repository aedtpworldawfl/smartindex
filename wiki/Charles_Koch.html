Who is Charles Koch

Charles de Ganahl Koch is an American business founder and philanthropist. He is the chairman and CEO of Koch Industries, one of the largest private companies in the United States.

He and his late brother David Koch expanded their father's business into a diversified conglomerate.

Early Life and Education

Charles Koch was born on November 1, 1935 in Wichita, Kansas. His father Fred C. Koch was a chemical engineer who founded what became Koch Industries.

He studied at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), earning bachelor's and master's degrees in chemical engineering.

Career

Koch joined his father's company in 1961 and became chairman and CEO in 1967 after his father's death. He transformed Koch Industries from a mid-size oil company into a conglomerate with interests in refining, chemicals, paper, and investments.

The company owns brands like Georgia-Pacific, Invista and Molex.

He is also known for libertarian political activism and philanthropy through Koch Foundation.

Impact and Recognition

Koch built Koch Industries into one of the largest private companies globally with over $100 billion in annual revenue.

He is a major donor to educational, political and cultural causes and co-authored books on business management.
