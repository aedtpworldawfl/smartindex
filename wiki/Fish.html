Overview

Fish is a water animal built for swimming and breathing underwater and available worldwide in rivers, lakes and oceans.

Features

  • Swims in water
  • Breathes with gills
  • Has fins and scales
  • Eats small plants and insects
  • Lives in groups called schools

History

Lived in water for millions of years, one of the oldest animals, now kept in aquariums and caught for food.
