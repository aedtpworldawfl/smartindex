What are Ghana Digital Centres and Ghana Innovation Hub

Accra Digital Centre (ADC) is a technology park in Accra that hosts BPOs, startups, tech hubs, government digital agencies, and innovation labs.

Ghana Innovation Hub is the startup hub inside ADC that supports startups under Ghana Tech Lab and MasterCard Foundation.

ADC is the heart of Digital Ghana - houses Cyber Security Authority, Data Protection Commission, Ghana Domain Name Registry, E-Transform Project.

History

  • 2014: World Bank approves $8M for Ghana E-Transform Project - includes digital centre
  • 2015: President Mahama commissions Phase 1 - 12 warehouses of former State Transport Company converted
  • 2019: Vice President Bawumia commissions Phase 2 - extra 6,000 sqm
  • 2018: Ghana Innovation Hub launched inside ADC with MasterCard Foundation - GHS 10M support
  • 2021: Cyber Security Authority and Data Protection Commission move to ADC
  • 2024: 40+ companies operating inside - BPOs, startups, call centres

Facilities at Accra Digital Centre

1. Office Spaces - 20 warehouses converted to offices - cheap rent - $10 per sqm
2. Ghana Innovation Hub - co-working, training rooms, makerspace, 500 capacity
3. Data Centre - Tier 2 data centre - backup for government systems
4. Conference Centre - 1,000 capacity hall for events - Ghana Tech Summit, Startup Festival
5. BPO Centre - Business Process Outsourcing companies - call centres serving US, UK
6. Incubation Labs - Ghana Tech Lab AI lab, Makerspace with 3D printers

Companies and Agencies at ADC

Government Agencies

  • Cyber Security Authority (CSA) - headquarters
  • Data Protection Commission (DPC)
  • Ghana Digital Centres Limited (GDCL) - manager
  • NITA - National Information Technology Agency
  • GIFEC - Ghana Investment Fund for Electronic Communications

Tech Companies and BPOs

  • Ameyo - BPO - 200 jobs
  • DynaPharm - e-commerce
  • Hubtel - e-commerce and messaging
  • mPharma - healthtech - inside ADC early
  • 15+ call centres - employing SHS graduates

Hubs and Startups

  • Ghana Tech Lab - main office
  • Ghana Innovation Hub - startups
  • iSpace, MEST startups

Ghana Innovation Hub Programs

  • Base Innovation Program - 6-month training for startups - Ghana Hubs Network
  • PATH - Pioneering and Aligned Tech Hub - MasterCard Foundation and World Bank - trained 10,000 youth in digital skills
  • Young Africa Works - MasterCard Foundation - jobs for youth
  • Ghana Startup Incubation - under Startup Act

Trained over 20,000 youth - 60% women.

Kumasi Digital Centre and Regional Centres

Government plans regional digital centres:

  • Kumasi Digital Centre - near KNUST - planned - $5M
  • Tamale Digital Centre - planned under Northern Development
  • Takoradi Digital Centre - Western Region - planned

But only Accra centre is operational as of 2025.

Impact

  • 3,000 direct jobs created - BPO agents, developers, security
  • 10,000+ youth trained
  • 100+ startups incubated
  • Reduced cost for startups - cheap internet via NITA fibre - $50 per month vs $300 outside
  • Attracts investors - World Bank, GIZ visit ADC to see ecosystem

Challenges

  • Dumsor - light out affects centre - needs generator - diesel cost high - your concern
  • Internet - though better than outside, still slow at times
  • Maintenance - warehouses old - leaking roof during rains
  • Location - Ring Road traffic - hard to access
  • Funding - GDCL struggles to maintain
