Deer

Deer
TypeMammal
Found inForests and grasslands of Asia, Europe, Africa, North America and South America
DietHerbivore

Deer is a graceful plant-eating animal with long legs. Deer belong to the family Cervidae.

Male deer usually grow antlers made of bone, which they shed and regrow every year. Reindeer, also called caribou, are unusual because females grow antlers too.

Kinds of deer

Well-known kinds are the red deer, the white-tailed deer, the moose and the reindeer.

Family

A baby deer is called a fawn. Fawns often have spots that help them hide in the grass.

Facts

  • Deer eat grass, leaves, buds and fruit.
  • They run fast and jump high to escape danger.
