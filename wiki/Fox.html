Fox

Fox
TypeMammal
Found inEvery continent except Antarctica
DietOmnivore

Fox is a small to medium wild animal of the dog family with a pointed face, upright ears and a bushy tail.

The red fox is the most widespread kind and lives across much of the Northern Hemisphere. Other foxes include the Arctic fox, which has thick white winter fur, and the fennec fox, which has very large ears and lives in the desert.

Food

Foxes eat small animals such as mice and rabbits, as well as insects, fruit and eggs. They hunt mostly at dusk and at night.

Home and family

A fox rests and raises its young in a den. The babies are called kits, pups or cubs.

Facts

  • A fox's large ears help it hear small animals moving underground.
  • Foxes often live alone or in small family groups.
