Who is Bernard Arnault

Bernard Jean Étienne Arnault is a French business founder and investor. He is the chairman and CEO of LVMH, the world's largest luxury goods company.

He oversees brands including Louis Vuitton, Dior, and Moët & Chandon.

Early Life and Education

Bernard Arnault was born on March 5, 1949 in Roubaix, France. He studied engineering at École Polytechnique and graduated in 1971.

He began his career in his family's construction company.

Career

In 1984, Arnault acquired Boussac Saint-Frères which owned Christian Dior. In 1987, he became a major shareholder in LVMH and was appointed chairman in 1989.

Under his leadership, LVMH grew from a small group into a luxury empire with over 70 prestigious brands including Sephora, Givenchy, and Tiffany & Co..

He is known for strategic acquisitions and building global luxury dominance.

Impact and Recognition

Arnault transformed the luxury industry and made LVMH the most valuable luxury company in the world.

He is consistently ranked among the richest people globally and is a major art collector and philanthropist.
