Overview

Dog is a loyal domestic animal built for companionship, protection and work and available worldwide in homes and farms.

Features

  • Is loyal and friendly
  • Has sharp smell and hearing
  • Runs fast and plays
  • Helps humans with guarding and hunting
  • Lives with humans as a pet

History

Domesticated thousands of years ago from wolves, became the first domesticated animal, now lives with humans everywhere.
