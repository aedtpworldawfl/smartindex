Last.fm is a music website that tracks listening habits.

History

Last.fm was founded in 2002 in the United Kingdom. It introduced Audioscrobbler to track music listening.

Purpose

Last.fm recommends music based on listening history.

Notability

Last.fm is notable because its data is used by Spotify, MusicBrainz and BBC.
