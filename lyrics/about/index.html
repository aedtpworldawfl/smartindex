About LyricsWorld

LyricsWorld is the home for World (Worldwide) Music lovers — not just one country, the whole world. Find, read, and share lyrics from your favorite artists, wherever they're from and whatever language they sing in.

Our Mission

To build the biggest and most accurate lyrics platform for African music and World (Worldwide) music at large — where every artist has a home, every song has a page, and every lyric is verified by the people who actually made it.

How It Works

Artists publish and own their song pages, with full control over their lyrics and bio. Contributors help keep lyrics accurate by submitting corrections, which go through an Editor review queue before anything changes live. Admins and Editors keep the whole platform verified, organized, and running smoothly.

Verified Badges

Verified accounts and verified lyrics carry a small checkmark badge next to the name. Verification means LyricsWorld — or the artist themselves, for their own lyrics — has confirmed the content is accurate and official.

Availability

Worldwide. Anyone, anywhere, can read lyrics on LyricsWorld — no signup required.

Usage

Free for the World. Signing up is free for artists and contributors alike.
