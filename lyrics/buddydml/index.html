About Buddy DML

Buddy DML Emmanuel Deliver Amable (born 14 April 2008), known professionally as Buddy DML and also by the stage name The Trenchkid TM, is a Ghanaian musical artist, rapper, singer, songwriter, and record producer. His music is primarily influenced by hip hop, Afrobeat, and melodic trap styles. Artist Ghanaian musical artist, rapper, singer, songwriter, and record producer.
