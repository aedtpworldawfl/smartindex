Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Go to Sign Up and choose whether you're a Contributor (submit lyric corrections) or an Artist (publish your own songs). Only Artists get a public artist page and song folder.

How do I publish a song?

Log in as an Artist, go to Dashboard > Submit Song, add a title, an optional 1:1 cover image URL, and your lyrics. Your song goes live immediately at /lyrics/your-username/song-title.

Can I edit a song after publishing it?

Yes. Artists can edit their own songs any time from My Songs. Editors and Admins can also edit any song on the platform.

What are the [Bracket] tags in lyrics for?

Wrapping a line in brackets — like [Chorus] or [Intro] — automatically turns it into a bold, blue section label, the same way section markers work on other lyrics sites.

How does copy-pasting into the editor work?

Anything pasted into a LyricsWorld editor panel — bio, lyrics, or corrections — is automatically stripped down to plain text first. Any styling from the original source is removed; you then apply LyricsWorld's own Bold, Italic, Underline, H1, H2, H3, or Paragraph formatting from the toolbar.

What do the verified badges mean?

A small badge next to a name means that account (or, for a song, its lyrics) has been confirmed accurate. Admins can verify or unverify any account. Artists can also verify their own lyrics.

I found a mistake in a song's lyrics. How do I fix it?

Sign up as a Contributor, then use Submit Correction (or "Suggest a Correction" on the song page itself). Your correction goes to an Editor for review before it replaces the published lyrics.

How long does a correction take to be reviewed?

Corrections sit in the Editor's Pending Queue and are usually reviewed within a few days. You'll see the change live on the song page once it's approved.

Can I use images that aren't hosted elsewhere?

Not yet — cover art and profile pictures are added as external image links (1:1 square images work best) rather than uploads.

Is LyricsWorld only for Ghanaian music?

No — LyricsWorld covers World (Worldwide) Music. Artists and lyrics from anywhere are welcome.

How do I change my password?

Log in, go to Dashboard > Change Password, and enter your current and new password.

I'm locked out and can't reset my own password. What now?

Email aedtpworld@gmail.com with your username and an Admin can reset it for you.
