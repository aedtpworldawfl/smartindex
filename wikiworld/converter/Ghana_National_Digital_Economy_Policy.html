What is Ghana National Digital Economy Policy

Ghana National Digital Economy Policy is a 6-year policy from 2024 to 2030 to transform Ghana into a digital economy.

It builds on Ghana Digital Agenda, Ghana Beyond Aid, and Ghana CARES program.

Launched to make Ghana the digital hub of West Africa, competing with Nigeria, Kenya, Rwanda.

History

Ghana started digitalisation in 2017 under Vice President Dr. Mahamudu Bawumia - Ghana Card, Mobile Money Interoperability, Paperless Ports, Ghana.gov.gh.

But no comprehensive digital economy policy until 2024.

World Bank funded Ghana Digital Acceleration Project - $200 million in 2022.

Policy drafted in 2023 with stakeholder consultations - hubs, telcos, banks, universities.

Launched in Accra in 2024.

5 Pillars of Policy

Pillar 1: Digital Infrastructure

  • 4G and 5G rollout - MTN, Telecel, AT have 5G license in 2024
  • Rural fibre - 2,000 km fibre for Eastern Corridor
  • National Data Centre upgrade
  • Accra Digital Centre Phase 2
  • Affordable internet - target 2% of income for 1GB

Pillar 2: Digital Skills and Jobs

  • Train 1 million youth in digital skills by 2030
  • Ghana Tech Lab, YALI, NEIP programs
  • Coding in basic schools - Ghana Education Service
  • 100,000 coders - Ghana Code Club

Pillar 3: Digital Entrepreneurship and Innovation

  • Startup Act implementation - Ghana Startup Fund
  • 50 hubs support - Ghana Hubs Network
  • Ghana Innovation Hub - Accra Digital Centre
  • Attract Big Tech - Google, Microsoft, Amazon presence in Ghana

Pillar 4: Digital Government and Services

  • Ghana.gov.gh - all government payments online
  • Ghana Card as single ID - NIA
  • Digital address - GhanaPostGPS
  • Paperless systems - DVLA, Passport Office, Registrar General
  • E-justice, E-health, E-education

Pillar 5: Digital Finance and Inclusion

  • Mobile Money Interoperability - GhIPSS
  • E-levy policy review to promote MoMo
  • National QR Code - GhQR
  • CBDC - eCedi by Bank of Ghana
  • Financial inclusion - 80% target by 2030

Key Projects Under Policy

1. Ghana.gov.gh - 2020 - single payment platform for government
2. Ghana Card - NIA - 17 million registered by 2024
3. Mobile Money Interoperability - 2018 - allows MTN, Telecel, AT, banks to transfer
4. GhanaPostGPS - digital address system
5. Smart Workplace - Government - e-government system
6. Accra Digital Centre - hub for BPO and startups
7. Rural Telephony Project - GIFEC - 2,000 rural sites

Economic Impact

  • Digital economy contributed 8% to GDP in 2023 - GSS
  • 150,000 digital jobs created - 2020-2024
  • Ghana ranked 4th in Africa for digitalisation - ITU 2024
  • Mobile Money transactions - 1.2 trillion GHS in 2023 - Bank of Ghana

Challenges

  • Cost of data - Ghana has expensive internet in Africa - $5 per 1GB vs $0.50 in Kenya
  • Dumsor / Light out - affects digital businesses - your experience yesterday
  • Digital divide - rural areas have low internet
  • Cybersecurity - scams and MoMo fraud increase
  • E-levy - some say it slows digital finance
