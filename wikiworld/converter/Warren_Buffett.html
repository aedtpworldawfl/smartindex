Who is Warren Buffett

Warren Edward Buffett is an American investor, business founder and philanthropist. He is the chairman and CEO of Berkshire Hathaway and one of the most successful investors in history.

He is known as the Oracle of Omaha.

Early Life and Education

Warren Buffett was born on August 30, 1930 in Omaha, Nebraska. He showed early interest in business and investing.

He studied at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln, Wharton School and Columbia Business School where he studied under Benjamin Graham.

Career

Buffett took control of Berkshire Hathaway, a textile company, in 1965 and transformed it into a diversified holding company owning companies like GEICO, Duracell and Dairy Queen.

He is famous for value investing strategy, focusing on long-term investments in undervalued companies.

He has pledged to give away most of his wealth through the Giving Pledge and Gates Foundation.

Impact and Recognition

Buffett is regarded as one of the greatest investors of all time. His annual shareholder letters are widely read in finance.

He has received the Presidential Medal of Freedom and is a leading philanthropist globally.
