What is Ghana Artificial Intelligence and Emerging Technologies

Artificial Intelligence (AI) in Ghana is use of AI, drones, blockchain, 3D printing, robotics, IoT to solve Ghanaian problems - health, agriculture, traffic, fraud.

Ghana is early stage - no national AI strategy yet - draft 2023 - but has world-leading drone delivery (Zipline) and strong AI research at KNUST.

History

  • 2017: Zipline starts talks with Ghana government
  • 2019: Vice President Bawumia launches Zipline Ghana - 4 drone centres - Omenako, Mampong, Vobsi, Sefwi Wiawso - delivers blood, vaccines, medicines - biggest in world - 1 million deliveries by 2024
  • 2019: Ghana Tech Lab AI program with GIZ - trains 10,000 youth in AI
  • 2020: Ghana AI Lab founded - private - by tech entrepreneurs
  • 2022: Responsible AI Lab (RAIL) launched at KNUST - $1M from IDRC Canada and GIZ - Prof. Jerry John Kponyo leads - focuses on responsible AI for Africa - AI for health, agriculture
  • 2023: Ministry of Communication drafts National AI Strategy - with UNESCO support
  • 2023: Ghana signs AU AI Continental Strategy

Key AI and Emerging Tech in Ghana

1. Zipline - Medical Drone Delivery - World Leader

  • Ghana has world's largest medical drone delivery network
  • 4 distribution centres - Eastern, Ashanti, North East, Western North
  • Drones fly 100km radius - 100km/h - deliver blood to hospitals - e.g., New Tafo Hospital - that saved mothers
  • Deliver vaccines - COVID-19 vaccines delivered via drone during pandemic - cold chain
  • Deliver snake anti-venom to rural clinics - critical for farmers
  • Operates with Ghana Health Service, GHS
  • 1 million deliveries by 2024 - 2,500 hospitals served
  • Cost: Government pays per delivery - $30M contract - controversial but saves lives

2. Artificial Intelligence Labs

  • Responsible AI Lab (RAIL) - KNUST - Kumasi - leader
* Projects: AI for crop disease detection - cassava, cocoa - farmers take picture - AI tells disease * AI for maternal health - predict complications * AI for Twi language - NLP for Ghanaian languages - Twi, Ga, Ewe - no Google Translate for Twi well * Lead: Prof. Jerry John Kponyo - Dean, Quality Assurance, KNUST
  • Ghana AI Lab - Accra - private - training
  • Ghana NLP - Open Source - Collective building language models for Ghanaian languages - Twi, Ga, Ewe, Dagbani - by Paul Azunre, Dr. Darlington Akogo - uses Mozilla Common Voice
  • Dr. Darlington Ahiale Akogo - Ghana AI pioneer - founder of minoHealth AI Labs, Runmila AI Institute - first Ghanaian to build AI for healthcare - cancer detection from scans - studied at KNUST

3. Blockchain

  • Ghana.gov.gh uses blockchain for land registry pilot - Bitland Ghana
  • Ghana Card uses blockchain-like verification - NIA
  • Bank of Ghana - eCedi - CBDC pilot - Central Bank Digital Currency - 2023 - to compete with MoMo - under development
  • Crypto trading - Binance, Yellow Card popular among youth - but BoG says crypto not legal tender - no regulation - many scams - Menzgold-like crypto scams

4. 3D Printing and Makerspaces

  • Ghana Tech Lab Makerspace at Accra Digital Centre - 3D printers, laser cutters
  • Kumasi Hive makerspace - Kumasi - builds IoT devices for farmers
  • 3D printed prosthetics for amputees - at Korle-Bu Teaching Hospital - NGO

5. Robotics

  • KNUST Robotics Club - builds robots for competitions - Pan-African Robotics Competition
  • Ashesi University robotics - Dr. Ayorkor Korsah leads
  • No industrial robots in Ghana - only educational

Startups Using AI in Ghana

  • Complete Farmer - AI for agriculture - founded by Desmond Koney - helps farmers
  • Farmerline - AI for farmers - voice in Twi - weather, prices - founded by Alloysius Attah
  • mPharma - AI for pharmacy supply chain
  • Kudobuzz - AI for e-commerce reviews
  • SnooCode - Digital address using QR - not GhanaPostGPS

National AI Strategy Draft 2023

Ministry of Communication and Digitalisation with UNESCO draft - key points:

1. AI for Agriculture, Health, Education priority
2. Train 100,000 youth in AI by 2030
3. Ghanaian language datasets - Twi, Ewe, Ga
4. Ethics - no AI for surveillance abuse
5. Hubs - AI hubs at universities - UG, KNUST, UCC, Ashesi

Strategy not yet launched as of 2025 - delayed.

Challenges

  • No data - AI needs data - Ghana has little digitized data - paper records
  • No computing power - need GPUs - expensive - light out - Dumsor kills servers
  • Brain drain - best AI talents move to US, UK, Canada - your friends
  • Internet cost - training AI needs internet - expensive
  • Language - no datasets for Twi, Ga - AI speaks English only
  • Funding - no VC for deep tech - investors want fintech only
