Emmanuel Deliver Amable (born 14 April 2008 in Anloga, Volta Region, Ghana) is a Ghanaian developer and musical artist. Emmanuel Deliver Amable is the founder of AEDTP WORLD and records music professionally as Buddy DML, also known as The Trenchkid TM.

Career

Emmanuel Deliver Amable began recording music in 2021 under the name Emmanuel Deliver DML, later shortened to Buddy DML. Between 2023 and 2024, Emmanuel Deliver Amable became active on digital platforms under the label AEDTP WORLD.

Emmanuel Deliver Amable is also the developer of AWFLWIKIWORLD, a lightweight database-free wiki engine built in PHP 8.3, and PROJECT BOOT, a browser-based IDE with DEV Mode.
