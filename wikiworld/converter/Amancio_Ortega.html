Who is Amancio Ortega

Amancio Ortega Gaona is a Spanish business founder and fashion entrepreneur. He is the founder of Inditex, the parent company of Zara, one of the world's largest fast fashion retailers.

He is known for pioneering the fast fashion model.

Early Life and Education

Amancio Ortega was born on March 28, 1936 in Busdongo, León, Spain. He moved to A Coruña as a child and left school early to work in clothing shops.

He started his career in garment making with his then-wife Rosalía Mera.

Career

In 1963, Ortega founded Confecciones Goa, a company making bathrobes. In 1975, he opened the first Zara store in A Coruña, Spain.

In 1985, he founded Inditex as the holding company for Zara and its other brands including Pull&Bear, Massimo Dutti and Bershka.

Under his leadership, Inditex grew into a global fashion empire with over 7,000 stores worldwide, revolutionizing fast fashion supply chain.

Impact and Recognition

Ortega transformed global retail with rapid design-to-store model. Zara can design, produce and deliver new clothing in weeks.

He is consistently ranked among the richest people in Europe and is known for his very private lifestyle.
