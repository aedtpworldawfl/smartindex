BBC is a British public service broadcaster.

History

BBC was founded in 1922 as the British Broadcasting Company. It is the world's oldest national broadcaster.

Purpose

BBC provides television, radio and online services.

Notability

BBC is notable because it is one of the largest broadcasters in the world and its content is used by Google, Spotify and MusicBrainz.
