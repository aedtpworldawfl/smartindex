MetaBrainz Foundation is a non-profit organization that operates MusicBrainz.

History

MetaBrainz Foundation was founded in 2004 to operate the MusicBrainz project.

Purpose

The foundation maintains MusicBrainz as an open music encyclopedia.

Notability

MetaBrainz is notable because its data is used by Spotify, BBC, SpotifyWorld and Google.
