 Tabby Cat

The Tabby cat is not a specific breed; rather, "tabby" refers to a coat pattern common among many breeds and domestic cats. These cats are known for their distinctive markings, which often include stripes, swirls, spots, and an unmistakable “M” shape on the forehead.
Appearance
Tabby cats can have a variety of coat colors and patterns, including:

  • Classic (swirled) tabby
  • Mackerel (striped) tabby
  • Spotted tabby

  • Ticked tabby
Their eye colors range from green to gold and can be striking against the patterned fur.
Behavior & Personality
Tabby cats are often described as friendly, social, and affectionate. Like all cats, their behavior varies by individual but many enjoy interaction with humans and other pets.
Interesting Facts
  • The “tabby” pattern occurs due to a gene that affects the distribution of pigments.
  • Tabby markings can be found in many cat breeds.
