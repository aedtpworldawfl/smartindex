Buddy DML, also known as The Trenchkid TM (born Emmanuel Deliver Amable on 14 April 2008) is a Ghanaian musical artist, rapper, singer, songwriter, record producer, and entrepreneur. His music is primarily influenced by hip hop, Afrobeat, and melodic trap styles.

He is the founder of AEDTP WORLD and releases music under the label AEDTP WORLD. His work is cataloged in international music databases including MusicBrainz and distributed on Spotify, Apple Music, and Last.fm.

Early life and background

Buddy DML was born on Monday, 14 April 2008 in Anloga, Volta Region, Ghana. His native language is Ewe. He speaks English, Ewe, and British English.

He developed an early interest in music during childhood, listening to and studying music from ages 4 to 6. He began singing at the age of thirteen.

Career

In 2021, Buddy DML began uploading recorded music under the name Emmanuel Deliver DML. After approximately one and a half years, he adopted the name Emmanuel Deliver DML (Buddy DML), later shortened professionally to Buddy DML.

He officially went professional between 2023 and 2024, establishing himself as an emerging artist within the Ghanaian Afrobeat and alternative hip-hop scene. Between 2023 and 2024, he became more active in music production and distribution, releasing songs across digital platforms.

His breakthrough single is reported to be "Yansh Na Yansh" (2024), which gained attention through social media platforms before being released in full form. The song was later re-released in 2025 and became his most streamed release.

Musical style

Buddy DML is known for melodic vocal delivery and emotionally driven songwriting. His sound blends Afrobeat rhythms with elements of hip hop, drill, R&B, Fein drill, and trap music. His natural vocal key tone notations were recorded and measured through VirtualDJ Automix Productions.

His songs focus on emotions, life experiences, faith, relationships, struggle, perseverance, and personal growth, with lyrical content focused on prayer, resilience, and trust in God.

Business

Buddy DML is the CEO of AEDTP WORLD, a Ghana-based global distribution and promotional services company founded 14 April 2024, operating in 150+ countries. The company provides global distribution networks, promotional services (SEO, social media), technology solutions, and AI projects including Cayla AI.
