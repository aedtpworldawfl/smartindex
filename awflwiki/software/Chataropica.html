Chataropica is a username-first chat app. Users do not need a phone number to chat. Every user has a username and a chat link. To chat with someone, you use chat.aedtpworld.com/?with=username.

Chataropica allows personal, business and artist accounts. Personal accounts are for regular users. Business accounts are for businesses. Artist accounts are for creators and public figures.

The app has a verification system. Green verification is for real people. Black verification is for businesses. Blue verification is for official artists and partners.

Features of Chataropica include text messaging, image sharing, user search and discovery, online status, day theme and night theme, external URL avatars, and direct chat links.

The design of Chataropica is simple and fast. It is made to work well even with slow internet. It is lightweight and easy to use.
