Audiomack (Audiomack Inc., American music company) is a music streaming and audio discovery platform that allows artists to upload music and listeners to stream and download for free.

Audiomack was founded in 2012 in New York by Dave Macli, David Ponte, Brian Zisook and others. It started as a place for hip-hop DJs and artists to share mixtapes without pop-up ads and malware. In 2013, artists like J. Cole and Chance The Rapper released projects exclusively on Audiomack.

The platform grew as an artist-first service. Any artist can upload without limits, and fans can stream and download offline for free. It makes money from advertising and subscriptions, not from charging artists to upload.

Audiomack became especially important in Africa from 2019. While other streaming services focused on Western markets, Audiomack was adopted widely in Nigeria, Ghana, Tanzania, Senegal, and Kenya. As of 2021, it was the top-ranked music streaming app on iOS in those countries. It has presence in all 54 African countries and has offices and teams in Nigeria and Ghana.

The service focuses on hip-hop, R&B, Afrobeats, Latin, and emerging genres. It operates playlist systems like Now Trending and curated Afrobeats playlists that help new artists get discovered.

Audiomack has licensing deals with Warner Music Group, Universal Music Group, Empire, and others. In 2020 it launched AMP, the Audiomack Monetization Program, which allows artists to earn from streams. It also launched Supporters, where fans can directly support artists.

As of 2024, Audiomack has over 20 million monthly users globally and over 225 million app downloads. It is available on Web, Android, iOS, and desktop platforms.

Audiomack is significant because it democratized music distribution by making upload free and offline listening free, which made it accessible in emerging markets where data costs are high.
