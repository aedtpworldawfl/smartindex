AEDTP WORLD is a Ghana-based global business development and technology networking company founded by Emmanuel Deliver Amable on April 14, 2024. Headquartered in Anloga, Volta Region, Ghana, it is described as a multidisciplinary creative-technology and distribution company empowering businesses, creators, and musicians.

The company operates under the tagline "Discover And Connect" and uses the AEDTP WORLD FREE LICENSE (AWFL) as a free, open digital ecosystem.

Company overview

AEDTP WORLD is listed as a global leader in distribution and promotional services with presence in over 150 countries across North America, South America, Europe, Africa, Asia and Oceania. Company details verified on SoundBetter, Music In Africa, and Simple Wikipedia:

  • Name: AEDTP WORLD
  • Headquarters: Ghana
  • Founded: 14th April 2024
  • Mission: To provide innovative and cost-effective solutions for businesses, creators, and musicians to achieve global success
  • Logo: Globe-shaped design with blue circle, blue oval, two parallel lines, and five dots

Founder

Emmanuel Deliver Amable, born April 14, 2008, is known professionally as Buddy DML and The Trenchkid TM. He is a Ghanaian musical artist, rapper, singer, songwriter, record producer, technology enthusiast, software and web developer. He began singing and entrepreneurial journey at age 13. Despite financial challenges, he established AEDTP WORLD to make global impact.

Services Offered

1. Global Distribution Networks: Operating in over 150 countries across six continents, ensuring timely and efficient product delivery.

2. Promotional Services: SEO, social media marketing, lead generation, digital PR.

3. Technology Solutions: State-of-the-art logistics, supply chain management, web and app development, customer support.

4. Artificial Intelligence (AI) Solutions: Developed first AI in January 2025 – AEDTP WORLD AI and Cayla AI – to assist in education, customer support, multilingual tasks. Ecosystem includes Blue Melody, CHEMOLAB, Project Boot Dev, PHYHISTORY, VIDITALL, all under AWFL.

Industries Served

  • Music: Distribution and promotion for musicians and record labels
  • E-commerce: Product distribution and marketing
  • Entertainment: Content distribution for film, TV, gaming
  • Education: Services for institutions, publishers, EdTech – including HISRC HiSchool system powered by AEDTP WORLD in partnership with CrossTech Ghana, supporting schools like Abor SHS, Adidome SHS, Akatsi SHS
