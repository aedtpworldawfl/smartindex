Help — How to use AWFL WIKI

Getting started

Create a free account, then use Write & Publish to choose a category and start your first article. Every article you publish counts toward your rank and verified badge.

Writing an article

The editor supports text formatting, paragraphs, fonts, and inserting images, audio and video by URL. Pick the category that best fits your topic before publishing — this can't be changed later, but the title and content can always be edited afterward.

Verified badge & ranks

Your rank rises for every 10 articles you publish. Reaching rank 1 unlocks the verified badge, and each rank above that shows as "+N" beside it. Top-ranked contributors surface first across the site.

Claiming an account

If you already have a username reserved for you, use Claim Account, search your username, and enter your 12-character claim code to gain access and set a new password.

Editing & deleting

You can edit or delete only what you created; admins can moderate any article. Deleting your account removes all of your data permanently, so we'll always ask you to confirm first.

Still need help?

Email us directly and we'll get back to you.
