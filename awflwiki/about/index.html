About AWFL WIKI

AEDTP WORLD FREE LICENSE (AWFL) WIKI is an advanced wiki media platform under AEDTP WORLD for sharing articles and data — an open encyclopedia across animals, artists, books, business, country, creative, dictionary, education, nature, people, schools, software and technology.

Anyone can create a free account and publish articles; the community's most active contributors earn verified badges and rise through the ranks.
