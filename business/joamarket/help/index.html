Help Center

Buying

Create a free customer account and your name, email, WhatsApp, gender and phone number are saved securely. When you check out, that information is used automatically — you won't be asked to fill in a form again.

Selling

Open the Business Portal to sign in or create a business account. Once signed in you can publish products, and every order that comes in is recorded privately in your own Customers & Orders page.

Claiming an existing account

If your business was set up for you and you were issued a 12-character claim code, use the Claim page from the Business Portal to gain access.
