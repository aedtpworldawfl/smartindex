Buddy DML is a Ghanaian musical artist born Emmanuel Deliver Amable on 14 April 2008 in Anloga, Volta Region, Ghana. He is known professionally as Buddy DML and also as The Trenchkid TM.


Early Life

He was born in Anloga. He developed interest in music as a child. He started singing at age 13. He speaks Ewe and English.

Career

He began recording music in 2021 under the name Emmanuel Deliver DML. He later changed his name to Buddy DML. From 2023 to 2024 he became more active in releasing music on digital platforms like Spotify, Apple Music, Boomplay, Audiomack. He releases his music under his own label AEDTP WORLD where he is the founder and CEO.


His music is cataloged on international databases like MusicBrainz, Last.fm, SoundBetter.

Musical Style

His style mixes Afrobeat rhythms with hip hop, trap, drill and R&B. He uses melodic delivery. His lyrics talk about struggle, love, faith, ambition, street life, emotional growth, relationships and personal experiences.


Breakthrough

His breakthrough song is Yansh Na Yansh. It was first released 18 May 2024. It gained attention on TikTok and other social platforms and was re-released in 2025. It established him in the Ghanaian digital music scene.
